# Signed Arithmetic

On a number line, the opposite of x is −x. Subtraction is defined by a−b=a+(−b); equal signs in multiplication/division give a positive result and unlike signs give a negative result. The rules preserve familiar distributive and associative relationships and extend to fractions.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 119-140.
