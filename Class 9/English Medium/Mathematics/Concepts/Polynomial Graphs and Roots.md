# Polynomial Graphs and Roots

Plot ordered pairs (x,p(x)) to represent a polynomial. A root is an x-value where p(x)=0, shown by intersection with the horizontal axis. Linear polynomials graph as straight lines; quadratic examples curve and may have zero, one or two real roots.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 209-222.
