# Circumference and Pi

The circumference-to-diameter ratio is constant for every circle and is denoted π. Thus C/d=π, C=πd=2πr. The textbook approximates π by comparing inscribed and circumscribed regular polygons; additional sides narrow the bounds.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 151-156.
