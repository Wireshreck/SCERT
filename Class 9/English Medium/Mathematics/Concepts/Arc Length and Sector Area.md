# Arc Length and Sector Area

For central angle θ degrees, arc length is θ/360 × 2πr and sector area is θ/360 × πr². The fraction of a full turn is the same fraction of circumference and circle area.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 185-194.
