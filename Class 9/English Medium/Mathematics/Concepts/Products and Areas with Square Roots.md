# Products and Areas with Square Roots

For nonnegative a,b, √a·√b=√(ab); conversely √(ab)=√a√b. A square of side √a has area a. Similar geometric decompositions connect areas of squares, rectangles and triangles to expressions containing roots; decimal bounds provide practical approximations.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 83-98.
