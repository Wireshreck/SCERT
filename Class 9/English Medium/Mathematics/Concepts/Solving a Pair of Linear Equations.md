# Solving a Pair of Linear Equations

Represent two unknown quantities with two linear equations. Eliminate one variable by adding/subtracting suitable multiples of the equations, solve the remaining one-variable equation, and substitute back. Interpret the ordered pair in the original context and verify both equations.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 7-18.
