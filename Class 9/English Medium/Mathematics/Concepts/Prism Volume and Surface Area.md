# Prism Volume and Surface Area

For a prism with base area B, base perimeter P and perpendicular height h, volume V=Bh and lateral surface area L=Ph. Total surface area adds the areas of the two congruent bases: S=Ph+2B. For a cylinder, B=πr² and P=2πr.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 195-208.
