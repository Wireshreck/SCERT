# Irrational Numbers and Decimal Bounds

A number is irrational when it cannot be written as a ratio of integers. The book motivates such values through square diagonals and areas. To approximate √n, locate consecutive decimal numbers whose squares lie on either side of n, then refine the interval one decimal place at a time.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 19-32.
