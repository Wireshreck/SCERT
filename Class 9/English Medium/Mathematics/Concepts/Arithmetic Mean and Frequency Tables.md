# Arithmetic Mean and Frequency Tables

For observations x₁,…,xₙ, mean = (Σxᵢ)/n. With values xᵢ and frequencies fᵢ, mean = (Σfᵢxᵢ)/(Σfᵢ). In grouped tables, a representative value such as the class midpoint is used, so state the approximation assumption.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 233-242.
