# Similarity and Scale Factor

Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides. If the scale factor from a smaller triangle to a larger one is k, corresponding lengths multiply by k and areas by k². Match vertices consistently before writing ratios. The parallel-line criterion and right-triangle constructions provide applications.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 99-118.
