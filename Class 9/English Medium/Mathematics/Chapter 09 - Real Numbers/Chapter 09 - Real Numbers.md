# Chapter 09 - Real Numbers

The number line extends beyond rational numbers to represent lengths such as circle circumferences and square-root values. Numbers are ordered by position. Absolute value gives distance from zero; distance between two numbers is the absolute value of their difference. The midpoint of two points is the average of their coordinates. The chapter works with positive and negative rational values, irrational lengths and number-line geometry.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 165-176.
