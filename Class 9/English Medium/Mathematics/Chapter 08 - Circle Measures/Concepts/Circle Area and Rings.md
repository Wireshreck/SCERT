# Circle Area and Rings

Rearranging many narrow sectors approximates a rectangle with base πr and height r, so circle area A=πr². A ring or shaded region is found by subtracting the inner circle area from the outer circle area.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 157-164.
