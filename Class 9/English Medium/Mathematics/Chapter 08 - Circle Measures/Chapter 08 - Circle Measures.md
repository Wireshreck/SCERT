# Chapter 08 - Circle Measures

A circle’s circumference is developed by comparing it with perimeters of regular polygons placed around and inside it. The ratio of circumference to diameter is constant, π, so C=πd=2πr. Polygon perimeters converge toward the circle circumference as the number of sides increases. Area is developed by rearranging sectors into an approximate rectangle, yielding A=πr². The chapter applies these ideas to circles, rings, regular polygons and circle-inscribed/circumscribed figures, with Kerala architectural references and approximation examples.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 151-164.
