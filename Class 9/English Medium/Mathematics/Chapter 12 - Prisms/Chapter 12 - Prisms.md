# Chapter 12 - Prisms

A prism has congruent parallel polygonal bases and rectangular/parallelogram side faces; its cross-section remains constant along its length. The volume is base area multiplied by perpendicular height. Surface area is built from the base faces and lateral faces; for a right prism, lateral area is base perimeter times height. The unit develops triangular and polygonal prisms, cylinders as curved analogues, and nets/cut-and-fold models for volume and surface-area reasoning.
