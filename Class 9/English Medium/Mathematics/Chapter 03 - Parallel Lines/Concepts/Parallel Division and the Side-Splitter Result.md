# Parallel Division and the Side-Splitter Result

Parallel lines cut any two intersecting transversals into equal corresponding intervals. In a triangle, a line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio: if DE ∥ BC in △ABC, then AD/DB = AE/EC, equivalently AD/AB = AE/AC. The converse is used to test whether a segment is parallel.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 33-50.
