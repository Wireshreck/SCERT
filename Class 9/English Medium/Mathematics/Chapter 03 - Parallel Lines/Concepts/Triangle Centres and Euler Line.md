# Triangle Centres and Euler Line

The medians of a triangle meet at the centroid; the perpendicular bisectors meet at the circumcentre; the angle bisectors meet at the incentre; and the altitudes meet at the orthocentre. The chapter develops these centres with constructions and proves relationships among medians and midpoints. In a non-equilateral triangle the centroid, circumcentre and orthocentre lie on Euler’s line.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 51-60.
