# Polynomial Terms and Degree

A polynomial is a finite sum of terms with nonnegative integer powers of its variables. The coefficient is the numerical factor, and the degree is the largest exponent (for the single-variable expressions emphasized here). Examples include constant, first-degree and second-degree forms.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 177-184.
