# Chapter 10 - Polynomials

Measurement problems motivate expressions whose value changes with a variable. Terms combine numerical coefficients and powers of variables; the degree is the highest exponent. The chapter distinguishes constant, linear and quadratic forms and uses algebraic notation to express perimeter, area and volume changes. It introduces operations and interprets polynomial expressions from geometric dimensions.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 177-184.
