# New Numbers — Construction and Approximation

Use square/area constructions to identify irrational lengths, bracket square roots by decimal squares, refine approximations, and calculate perimeters using stated precision. Distinguish exact root expressions from rounded decimal values.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 25-32.
