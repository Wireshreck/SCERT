# Chapter 06 - Similar Triangles

Triangles with equal corresponding angles have proportional corresponding sides. A line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio; conversely, a proportional division establishes parallelism. The unit builds scale factors using corresponding sides, then applies them to lengths, areas, right triangles, projections and quadrilaterals. Diagrams show circumcircles and centres as well as geometric construction tasks; the exercises require identifying correspondence before setting up ratios.
