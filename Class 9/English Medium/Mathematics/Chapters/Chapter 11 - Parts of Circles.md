# Chapter 11 - Parts of Circles

A full rotation is 360 degrees. An arc’s length is the same fraction of the circumference as its central angle is of 360 degrees. A sector’s area is the same fraction of the circle’s area. The chapter models rotations, angles and sectors, then combines these relationships to find arc lengths and sector areas, including shaded figures formed from sectors and triangles.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 185-194.
