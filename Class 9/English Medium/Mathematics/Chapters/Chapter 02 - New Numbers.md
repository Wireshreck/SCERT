# Chapter 02 - New Numbers

This unit extends number representation through lengths and areas that cannot be expressed as rational fractions. Square-root notation names the positive number whose square is the given value; geometric dissection of squares motivates irrational lengths. Decimal approximations are developed by bracketing a root between nearby tenths, hundredths and finer values. The unit also uses irrational lengths in perimeter calculations and explains why approximations should match the precision of the context.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 19-32.
