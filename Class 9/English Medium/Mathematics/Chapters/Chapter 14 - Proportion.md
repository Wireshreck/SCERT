# Chapter 14 - Proportion

Proportional change compares two quantities through a constant ratio or constant multiplier. Direct proportion is represented by y=kx; inverse proportion by xy=k. Similar figures preserve side ratios while areas change by the square of the scale factor. The chapter tests proportionality in photographs, maps, triangles, circumference/angle relations, volume and other real contexts, and contrasts proportional with non-proportional changes.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 223-232.
