# Chapter 07 - Negative Numbers

A number-line model represents position and displacement, including movement in opposite directions. Integers extend arithmetic to negative displacement and temperature change. Addition combines signed displacements; subtraction is defined through addition of the opposite. Multiplication and division extend sign rules consistently, including products of two negatives. Tables and contexts (movement, speed and temperature) support the rules, followed by algebraic equations and operations with negative fractions.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 119-140.
