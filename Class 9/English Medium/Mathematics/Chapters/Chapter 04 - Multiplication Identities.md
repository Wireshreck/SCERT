# Chapter 04 - Multiplication Identities

Area models turn products into identities. Rectangles illustrate distributivity and expansions such as (a+b)(c+d); special cases yield the square of a sum, square of a difference, and product of sum and difference. The unit then examines products of consecutive and nearby natural numbers through calendar patterns and algebraic generalisation. Learners use identities both to expand expressions and to simplify numerical products.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 61-82.
