# Chapter 05 - Irrational Multiplication

Geometric area and length models motivate multiplication involving square roots. For positive a and b, √a√b=√(ab); square roots are approximated by decimal bounds when exact simplification is unavailable. The chapter uses squares, rectangles, right triangles and equilateral triangles to build and apply area relations, and relates the square of a sum/difference to expanded products. It includes worked calculations, diagrams, and exercises on lengths, areas and identities.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 83-98.
