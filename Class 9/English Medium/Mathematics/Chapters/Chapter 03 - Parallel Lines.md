# Chapter 03 - Parallel Lines

Parallel-line diagrams establish equal division: parallel lines cut transversals proportionally. The unit applies this principle to lengths in triangles and polygons, develops the side-splitter result for a line parallel to one side of a triangle, and studies the converse construction. Midpoints, medians, perpendicular bisectors, altitudes, angle bisectors, Euler-line relationships and triangle centres appear through constructions and proofs. Repeated diagrams and paper-folding/GeoGebra activities connect proportional lengths with area and congruence.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 33-60.
