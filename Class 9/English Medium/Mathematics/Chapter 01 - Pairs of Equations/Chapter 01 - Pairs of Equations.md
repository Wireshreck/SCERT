# Chapter 01 - Pairs of Equations

The opening unit models two related unknowns with a pair of linear equations. Everyday price and quantity stories are translated into equations, then solved by elimination: combine or subtract equations to remove one variable and substitute back for the other. The book emphasizes checking that a solution satisfies both equations, interpreting variables in context, and using algebra to reduce repeated arithmetic. Worked settings include prices, ages, lengths and counts; the exercises ask learners to formulate and solve systems.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 7-18.
