# Prism and cylinder surface area

Right prism lateral area L=P·h and total area S=P·h+2B. Cylinder lateral area L=2πr·h and total area S=2πrh+2πr².

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 201-208.
