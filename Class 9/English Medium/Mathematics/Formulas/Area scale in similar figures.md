# Area scale in similar figures

If corresponding side lengths have ratio k, then corresponding areas have ratio k². For triangles, area = ½ × base × perpendicular height; equal included angles plus proportional adjacent sides establish similarity in the textbook’s constructions.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 101-114.
