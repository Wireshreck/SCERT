# Signed operations

a−b=a+(−b). For multiplication/division, same signs produce a positive result and different signs a negative result. For a,b>0: (−a)(−b)=ab; (−a)b=−ab. Apply the same sign rules to fractions.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 123-140.
