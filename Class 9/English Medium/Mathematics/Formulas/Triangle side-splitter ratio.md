# Triangle side-splitter ratio

For DE ∥ BC in △ABC: AD/DB = AE/EC and AD/AB = AE/AC. The converse proportion can establish DE ∥ BC when the points lie on the corresponding sides.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 46-50.
