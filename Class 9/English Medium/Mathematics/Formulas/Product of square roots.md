# Product of square roots

For a≥0 and b≥0, √a × √b = √(ab), and √(a²)=a for a≥0. Keep nonnegative conditions explicit when simplifying principal square roots.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 84-92.
