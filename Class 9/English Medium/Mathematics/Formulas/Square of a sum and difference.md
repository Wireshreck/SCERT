# Square of a sum and difference

(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; (a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b². The middle term is twice the product of the two terms, with sign determined by the binomial.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 61-76.
