# Direct and inverse proportion

Direct: y=kx, so y₁/x₁=y₂/x₂=k. Inverse: xy=k, so x₁y₁=x₂y₂=k.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 223-232.
