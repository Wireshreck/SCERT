# Distance and midpoint on a number line

Distance(a,b)=|a−b|. Midpoint(a,b)=(a+b)/2. Absolute value is nonnegative and measures distance, irrespective of direction.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 168-176.
