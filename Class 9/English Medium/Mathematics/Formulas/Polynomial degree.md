# Polynomial degree

For p(x)=aₙxⁿ+…+a₁x+a₀ with aₙ≠0, degree(p)=n. The textbook examples focus on constant, first-degree and second-degree polynomials.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 179-184.
