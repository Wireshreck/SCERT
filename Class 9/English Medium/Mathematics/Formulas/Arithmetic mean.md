# Arithmetic mean

Ungrouped mean x̄=Σx/n. Frequency mean x̄=Σ(fx)/Σf. For grouped intervals, x is represented by the class midpoint; the estimate depends on this assumption.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 234-242.
