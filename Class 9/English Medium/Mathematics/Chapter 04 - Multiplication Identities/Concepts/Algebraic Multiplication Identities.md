# Algebraic Multiplication Identities

Area models support (a+b)(c+d)=ac+ad+bc+bd, (a+b)²=a²+2ab+b², (a−b)²=a²−2ab+b² and (a+b)(a−b)=a²−b². The identities are used to expand, factor and calculate products efficiently. Calendar arrangements illustrate products of consecutive numbers and generalisation.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 61-82.
