# Direct and Inverse Proportion

In direct proportion y/x=k (equivalently y=kx), so scaling x by a factor scales y by the same factor. In inverse proportion xy=k, so multiplying one variable by a factor divides the other by that factor. Check for a constant ratio/product across observations.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 223-232.
