# Chapter 15 - Statistics

Statistics condenses a large set of observations into useful summaries. The arithmetic mean is total value divided by count; frequency tables use the sum of value×frequency divided by total frequency. Graphs display values and departures from an average. Grouped data are handled by class intervals and representative values, with explicit assumptions where individual observations are unknown. Examples use cricket scores, wages, marks, rainfall, age and weight distributions.
