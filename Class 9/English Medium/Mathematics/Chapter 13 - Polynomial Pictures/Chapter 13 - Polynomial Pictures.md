# Chapter 13 - Polynomial Pictures

A polynomial can be represented by a table of values and a graph. Linear polynomials produce straight-line graphs; quadratic examples form curves. The graph makes roots (where the value is zero), intercepts, sign, growth and turning behaviour visible. The chapter uses plotted points, perpendicular constructions and GeoGebra screenshots to infer polynomial expressions and compare related graphs.

**Textbook reference:** Mathematics Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 209-222.
