# Energy Balance and Food Choice

Food supplies energy and nutrients; physical activity uses energy. The illustrated tables compare selected food calories with activity expenditure. A sustained mismatch can contribute to unhealthy weight change. The text encourages varied food, regular activity and reflection on lifestyle patterns rather than judging health from one meal.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 14-19.
