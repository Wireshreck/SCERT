# Posture and Kyphosis

Posture is body alignment while standing, sitting or moving. The text discusses kyphosis, its visible curvature, possible causes and signs such as back discomfort, and describes preventive habits and exercises. Correct movement, ergonomic habits and adult guidance support safe practice.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 33-39.
