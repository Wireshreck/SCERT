# Sport Culture, Inclusion and Socialisation

Sport culture comprises the values, customs and practices around participation, spectatorship and play. Inclusive sport adapts participation so different abilities and backgrounds can take part. Shared games build belonging, leadership, cooperation and respect when rules are fair and roles rotate.
