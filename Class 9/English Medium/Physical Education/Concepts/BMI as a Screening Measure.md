# BMI as a Screening Measure

The activity book defines Body Mass Index as body mass in kilograms divided by height in metres squared: BMI = mass(kg)/height(m)². It includes a reference chart for classroom interpretation. BMI is a screening indicator in this lesson; it is not a complete diagnosis of an individual’s health.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 21-23.
