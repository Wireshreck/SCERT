# Nature-Based and Adventure Activities

The chapter surveys land, water, air and snow activities, relating each to terrain and equipment. Trekking, kayaking, paragliding, mountain biking, ice skating and beach sports involve distinct environmental and safety requirements. Nature-friendly participation respects ecosystems and local conditions.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 57-66.
