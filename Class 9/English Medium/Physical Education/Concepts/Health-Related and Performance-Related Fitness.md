# Health-Related and Performance-Related Fitness

The chapter distinguishes fitness capacities associated with health from those supporting sport performance. It names agility (changing direction), balance (maintaining stability), coordination (combining movements), power (forceful movement), speed and reaction time. Games provide practice contexts.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 25-29.
