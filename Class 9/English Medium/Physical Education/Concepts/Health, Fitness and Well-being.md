# Health, Fitness and Well-being

Health includes physical, mental and social well-being. The chapter connects healthy habits, nutrition, activity, social support and emotional qualities with a satisfying life. Fitness is developed through regular participation, not a single performance.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 7-15.
