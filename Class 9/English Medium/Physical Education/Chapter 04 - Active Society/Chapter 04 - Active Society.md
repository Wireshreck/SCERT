# Chapter 04 - Active Society

Participation in sport can support physical development, coordination, teamwork, leadership, discipline, inclusion and social well-being. The chapter defines sport culture through values and everyday practices and invites learners to identify barriers to participation. Cooperative games such as First the Leader, Circle of Friends, Captain Ball and Move as One teach shared rules, turn-taking, coordination and inclusive leadership. Activities ask students to plan improvements to participation in school and community.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 48-56.
