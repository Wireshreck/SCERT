# Chapter 02 - Achieve Fitness, Preserve Posture

Fitness involves health-related capacities and performance-related components such as agility, balance, coordination, power, speed and reaction time. Game activities let learners practise these skills. The chapter introduces yoga postures and benefits, then examines posture and deformities including kyphosis, with symptoms, possible causes, prevention and corrective exercise guidance. Illustrations support posture checks and movement sequences; the exercises encourage observing safe form and regular practice.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 24-39.
