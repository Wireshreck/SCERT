# Chapter 03 - The Safety Ring of Care

The chapter develops prevention and first-response awareness for water-related accidents, drowning and choking. It emphasizes avoiding unsafe water entry, calling for help, and following safe first-aid steps illustrated in the book. Swimming is presented for fitness, recreation and life-saving capability. Classroom games reinforce communication and safety decisions. The content distinguishes prevention, rescue and first aid, and stresses adult/emergency assistance.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 40-47.
