# Chapter 05 - Knowing Nature through Games

Outdoor and adventure activities connect physical education with place, landscape and environmental awareness. The chapter introduces trekking, water sports, kayaking, paragliding, mountain biking, ice skating and beach sports through illustrated examples and short descriptions. Learners compare activities, identify equipment and safety considerations, and observe local natural features. The closing tasks ask students to design nature-friendly games and reflect on responsible participation.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 57-66.
