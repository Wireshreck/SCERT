# Agility, Balance and Coordination

Agility is the ability to change direction quickly; balance is the ability to maintain control of body position; coordination is the ability to combine body movements smoothly.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 26.
