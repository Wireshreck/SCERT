# Body Mass Index

A ratio relating body mass to squared height: BMI = mass in kilograms ÷ (height in metres)². The activity book provides a chart for classroom discussion.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 22.
