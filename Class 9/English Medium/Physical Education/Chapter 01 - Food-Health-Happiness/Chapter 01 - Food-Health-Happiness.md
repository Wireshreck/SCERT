# Chapter 01 - Food-Health-Happiness

The chapter connects well-being with balanced nutrition, regular physical activity, emotional health and social relationships. A story about a young athlete introduces persistence and achievement; a cricket biography shows how sustained effort supports performance. Illustrated activities include games, dance and calorie-intake/activity tables. Learners compare energy from foods with energy used during activities, consider lifestyle risks and disease prevention, and reflect on healthy routines. The textbook presents a BMI calculation and chart as a classroom learning tool.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 7-23.
