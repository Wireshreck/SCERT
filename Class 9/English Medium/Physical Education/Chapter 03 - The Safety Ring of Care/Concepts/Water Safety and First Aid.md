# Water Safety and First Aid

Prevention comes first: avoid entering unsafe water, use appropriate supervision and seek trained help. The book’s drowning-response sequence emphasizes checking safety, calling emergency help, supporting the person from a safe position, and following first-aid/CPR guidance only when trained. Choking content likewise separates prevention from prompt first aid and emergency response.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 40-47.
