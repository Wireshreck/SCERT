# Warm-up and Cool-down

Warm-up prepares the body for activity and helps transition into more vigorous movement. Cool-down follows activity, allowing a gradual return toward rest. The illustrated sequences show controlled stretches, walking/jogging and relaxation; movements should be performed smoothly without forcing painful ranges.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 71-84.
