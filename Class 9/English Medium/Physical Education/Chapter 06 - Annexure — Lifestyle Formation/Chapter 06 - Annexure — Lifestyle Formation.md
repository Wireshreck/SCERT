# Chapter 06 - Annexure — Lifestyle Formation

The annexure turns health learning into a daily routine: nutritious food, regular movement, appropriate screen habits, study/rest balance and sleep. It illustrates warm-up movements before activity and cool-down stretches afterward, with step-by-step photographs. The sequence includes upper-body, trunk and lower-body stretches, walking/jogging and relaxation. Follow the activity book’s teacher guidance and adjust movement to safe individual ability.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 67-84.
