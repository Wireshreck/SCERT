# Achieve Fitness — Games, Yoga and Posture

Games practise agility, balance, coordination, power, speed and reaction time. Yoga/posture tasks use illustrated steps, observation and reflection. Learners identify kyphosis prevention and safe movement habits; follow teacher guidance and do not force painful positions.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 25-39.
