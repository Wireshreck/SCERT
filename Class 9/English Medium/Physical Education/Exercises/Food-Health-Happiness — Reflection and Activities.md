# Food-Health-Happiness — Reflection and Activities

Activities examine persistence and achievement, explore food and activity calories, compare daily habits, and reflect on physical, emotional and social components of health. The book includes tables and a BMI activity; use these as learning prompts, not diagnoses.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 8-23.
