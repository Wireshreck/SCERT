# Safety Ring — Prevention and Response

Review the book’s prevention advice, identify safe responses to drowning/choking situations and distinguish calling for help, rescue and trained first aid. Swimming and safety games reinforce discussion. Use adult/emergency support in real incidents.

**Textbook reference:** Health and Physical Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 41-47.
