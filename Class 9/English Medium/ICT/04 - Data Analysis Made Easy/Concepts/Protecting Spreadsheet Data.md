# Protecting Spreadsheet Data

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 4 · printed p. 56**

Calc’s Protect Sheet feature can limit unauthorized changes while still allowing a spreadsheet to be opened and viewed. The chapter demonstrates entering and confirming a password for the sheet, then asks learners to investigate editing a protected sheet and how to regain editing access. It also invites them to explore protection for selected cells rather than an entire sheet.

Protection is an access-control step for the worksheet’s contents; it does not replace saving the workbook.
