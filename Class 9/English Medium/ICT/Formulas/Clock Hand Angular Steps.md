# Clock Hand Angular Steps

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 3 · printed pp. 41–43**

A full turn around the clock face is 360°.

- **Second hand:** 360° ÷ 60 seconds = **6° per second**.
- **Minute hand:** 360° ÷ 60 minutes = **6° per minute**.
- **Hour hand:** 360° ÷ 12 hours = **30° per hour**. It advances 30° ÷ 60 = **0.5° per minute** as the minute hand moves.

The textbook expresses the hour-hand direction as:

`Point in Direction = Current Hour × 30 + Current Minute × 0.5`

This accounts for the hour hand’s gradual motion between hour marks. The values are used in PictoBlox’s direction instructions in Figures 3.4–3.6.
