# Population Density

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 4 · printed pp. 50–51**

Population density is the average number of people per square kilometre of an area.

`Population density = total population ÷ land area`

In the textbook’s Calc table, the example for Uttar Pradesh divides the population in cell `C2` by the land area in `F2`, entering `=C2/F2` in the population-density column (Figure 4.2, p. 50). The fill handle applies the formula to the other state rows. The chapter then rounds the values to two decimal places before categorizing them.
