# Chapter 6 — Intelligent Machines

**Standard IX · English Medium · ICT · printed pp. 7–19**

The chapter introduces artificial intelligence (AI) through a forest-surveillance example. Drone and camera images can be analysed to detect, identify, count, and monitor animals. It then explains AI models, natural-language processing (NLP), prompts and prompt refinement, AI-generated images, educational uses, responsible-use principles, and AI agents. Page illustrations include a wildlife-surveillance news clipping, tables describing AI and NLP stages, example AI responses to prompts, and an AI-generated picture (pp. 7–18).

## Concepts and relationships

- [[../Concepts/AI Models and Pattern Recognition]] — data collection, pattern recognition, and decisions; a model is trained on examples for a task (pp. 8–9).
- [[../Concepts/Natural Language Processing]] — NLP systems interpret language, analyse structure, and generate responses; examples include assistants and chatbots (pp. 10–11).
- [[../Concepts/Prompt Engineering]] — clear task, audience, context, examples, and iterative refinement shape responses (pp. 11–14).
- [[../Concepts/AI Image Generation]] — image systems create visuals from text prompts, with re-prompting where the result misses the intent (pp. 14–16).
- [[../Concepts/Responsible AI and AI Agents]] — check output, protect privacy, avoid harm, credit assistance, and understand agent coordination (pp. 16–18).

## Learning activities and assessment

The chapter asks learners to compare general and detailed explanations, refine a prompt for an age-appropriate audience, create a story and poem, generate an image for a poster, identify AI systems for different tasks, and prepare a table of Kerala Chief Ministers and their tenures using an NLP system. Assessment asks about responsible-AI principles and the defining feature of an AI agent (printed p. 19). See [[../Exercises/Chapter 06 - Intelligent Machines]].

## Key terminology

Artificial intelligence (AI), model, data collection, pattern recognition, decision making, natural-language processing (NLP), large language model (LLM), prompt, prompt engineering, AI agent.
