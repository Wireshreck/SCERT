# Chapter 7 — Activities and Assessment

**Printed pp. 21–34.** Activity summary; no textbook answers are invented.

- Identify the GeoGebra tools used to create points, lines, polygons, circles and angles (p. 21).
- Construct equally spaced parallel lines and observe the segments cut by a transversal (p. 22).
- Use a triangle applet to compare side ratios when a line is parallel to one side; drag a vertex to test the relationship (p. 23).
- Construct a 19-unit segment divided into three equal parts and save it (pp. 23–24).
- Use GeoGebra to create triangles, rectangles, squares and parallelograms; complete the tool-identification assessment and follow-up construction tasks (p. 24).
- In Kalzium, compare element counts across groups and periods; inspect sodium’s element details and atom model; record properties of listed elements in the tables (pp. 25–31).
- Use Kalzium to classify main-group elements and identify families, states and uses; answer the atom-model assessment and research two everyday-use chemicals (pp. 28–31).
- In PhET Bending Light, observe the path through different media and prisms, measure angles, complete the media/angle table and compare the assessment diagrams (pp. 32–34).
