# Virtual Laboratories and Light Refraction

Scientific simulation software recreates a phenomenon on a computer so learners can investigate it without carrying out the physical activity. The chapter uses PhET’s **Bending Light** simulation to study how a ray changes direction between media (printed pp. 32–34; Figs. 7.11–7.12).

Learners move the light source, choose media, inspect ray or wave views, and use a protractor to measure the angle of incidence and angle of refraction. The table gives an example of light from air to water with incidence angle 45° and refraction angle 30° (Table 7.6, p. 34). The text concludes that when light travels from air into water the path bends closer to the normal; when the media are the same, its direction does not change (p. 34).
