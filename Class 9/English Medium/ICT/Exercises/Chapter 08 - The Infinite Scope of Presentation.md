# Chapter 8 — Activities and Assessment

**Printed pp. 35–48.** Activity summary; no answer key has been invented.

- Plan and create a presentation on bad habits and heart health, gathering information, images and video first (pp. 35–37).
- Insert an opening image, title and animation; design master slides with suitable typography, colours, shapes, and slide-number fields (pp. 36–41).
- Apply the master to other slides, preserving a consistent design, and create concise slides using bullets and appropriate charts (pp. 42–43).
- Convert the heart-risk data into an infographic with shapes, minimal text, title and image; arrange overlapping elements appropriately (pp. 43–46).
- Present the deck as a slide show to classmates (p. 46).
- Assessment: reason about applying a master to a populated slide, which properties masters control, and their purpose (p. 47).
- Follow-up: create a rain-conservation master slide, then an infographic from the supplied regional internet-penetration table (pp. 47–48).
