# Chapter 5 — The Web of Goodness: Assessment and Activities

**Standard IX · ICT · printed pp. 58–76**

## Practice embedded in the chapter

- Identify Wikipedia’s collaborative features and explore SchoolWiki school information (pp. 58–61).
- Create and edit a practice page in Ezhuthukalari; format text with headings and add an image (pp. 63–66).
- Classify social-media uses and discuss privacy/responsible participation (pp. 67–69).
- Complete the online transaction platform table and examine two-factor authentication (pp. 69–71).
- Explore the SAMAGRA Plus Learning Room and its subject resources (pp. 71–73).
- Discuss fact-checking, cybercrime types, and preventive measures (pp. 73–75).

## “Let’s Assess”

The questions review the software underlying Wikipedia/SchoolWiki, distinguish social media from SchoolWiki, and identify cyberstalking from a scenario involving information gathering, threats, and blackmail (p. 76). This note preserves the ideas tested without copying answer options.

## Extended activities

1. Add a book review and cover image to Ezhuthukalari.
2. Improve the “My Village” page in the SchoolWiki Projects area.
3. Watch the Biology “Biomolecules” resource in SAMAGRA Plus and answer its assessment.
4. Prepare a school seminar on safe conduct in cyberspace, using and checking reliable information.
