# SAMAGRA Plus Learning Room

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 71–73**

The textbook presents SAMAGRA Plus as a Kerala General Education Department portal for digital learning resources. Its Learning Room provides subject-related audio-visual materials, learning resources, assessment activities, self-assessment tools, and podcasts. Learners select their medium, class, and subject before exploring the available materials.

The chapter connects this portal with school learning during emergencies and with supplementary curriculum resources. One extended activity asks learners to use the Biology “Biomolecules” video and answer the linked assessment.
