# Data, Information and Spreadsheet Workbooks

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 4 · printed pp. 48–50**

**Data** are raw, unprocessed values or observations. Organizing and analysing those data can produce meaningful **information** that supports decisions and problem solving. In the chapter, population, land area, sex ratio, and literacy figures are organized in a Calc workbook to study differences among states.

A Calc spreadsheet file with multiple sheets is called a **workbook**. The chapter’s Census worksheet uses columns to organize measures and cells to store individual values; formulas in cells derive new information from those values.

The example relates population density to socio-economic conditions and resource sharing, connecting spreadsheet analysis with the Social Science discussion of demographic trends.
