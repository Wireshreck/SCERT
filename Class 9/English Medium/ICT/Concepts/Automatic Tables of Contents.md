# Automatic Tables of Contents

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 2 · printed pp. 34–36**

Writer can build a table of contents from heading styles instead of requiring the author to type entries and page numbers by hand. The generated list can reflect changes when pages or headings change. The author chooses the correct heading hierarchy so main headings and subheadings appear at the intended levels.

The chapter inserts a page break for the contents page, then uses **Insert → Table of Contents and Index**. Entries link to the corresponding sections; Ctrl-click follows an entry. The table can be updated or edited from its context menu.

**Connection:** heading styles (see [[Writer Styles and Document Consistency]]) are the structure the automatic contents feature uses to recognize and organize headings.
