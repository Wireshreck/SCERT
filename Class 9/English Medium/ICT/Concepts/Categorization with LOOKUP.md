# Categorization with LOOKUP

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 4 · printed pp. 51–53**

Categorizing values by criteria makes a large data table easier to interpret and search. The chapter divides population density into five named bands: very low (below 100), low (100 to less than 200), moderate (200 to less than 400), high (400 to less than 800), and very high (800 and above) people per square kilometre.

In Calc, the criteria are entered in a separate sheet, selected, and defined as a named range (`Criteria`). The LOOKUP function compares a state’s density with the criteria and returns its category. The formula can then be filled down the column to classify all states.

The criteria table is reusable: it separates the classification rules from each row of source data.
