# Network Types and Resource Sharing

A **computer network** connects computers so they can share information, hardware, and services. The chapter lists file transfer, Internet sharing, shared printers/scanners, and audio/video/screen sharing (printed pp. 62–63).

Networks are classified by coverage and use: **LAN** connects devices within a room or building (such as a school lab); **WAN** spans a country or larger area; **PAN** links personal devices around an individual (p. 66). Wireless networks connect devices without cables. The text describes Wi-Fi as suitable for a house or small office and Bluetooth as short range, with different typical speeds and device examples (p. 67).
