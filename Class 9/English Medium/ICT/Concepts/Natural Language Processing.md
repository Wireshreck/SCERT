# Natural Language Processing

**Natural-language processing (NLP)** is the area of AI concerned with computers understanding, interpreting, and responding to human language. The textbook divides an example NLP workflow into understanding a sentence by breaking it into words or phrases, analysing grammar and relationships, and creating a response or taking an action (printed pp. 10–11; Table 6.2, p. 10).

Language models predict words by detecting patterns in language; large language models are trained on very large text collections. The book gives chatbots, translation applications, assistants, and game characters as familiar NLP uses. A response can be general or more tailored depending on the question and context, so the examples compare different prompts about photosynthesis (pp. 10–13).

## Example and caution

An answer to “What is photosynthesis?” may be brief. Asking for an explanation of its light and dark phases can elicit more detail. The chapter also cautions that responses can vary and should be examined critically; it notes that Malayalam handling may not always work fully as expected (pp. 11–12, 17–18).
