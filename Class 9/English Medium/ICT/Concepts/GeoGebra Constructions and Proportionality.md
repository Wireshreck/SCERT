# GeoGebra Constructions and Proportionality

GeoGebra provides grouped tools for accurate geometric constructions. The textbook asks learners to identify tools for points, lines, polygons, circles and angles, then investigate geometric relationships by moving constructed objects (printed pp. 20–24).

In one activity, a transversal cuts equally spaced parallel lines; the displayed segments have equal lengths (Fig. 7.1, p. 22). In another, a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other sides proportionally: the figure gives \(EC/EA = FC/FB = 1.44\) (Fig. 7.2, p. 23). The dynamic construction lets learners drag vertices and compare ratios. A worked construction divides a 19-unit line into three equal parts by marking equal intervals on an auxiliary slanted line and drawing parallels (pp. 23–24).
