# Presentation Skills and Online Tools

Presentation skills help a speaker communicate clearly and effectively; the chapter says practice can gradually improve them (printed p. 36). A deck should organise ideas systematically and use text, images, and audio/video to simplify complex information and offer different communication styles (p. 35).

It identifies Google Slides, Prezi, Canva, and Slidego as collaborative or online presentation platforms, and mentions TEDx, YouTube and Toastmasters as ways to present ideas publicly (printed p. 46). AI systems can create decks quickly, but learners need basic presentation-software knowledge to correct them; the text recommends training in software such as Impress (p. 47).
