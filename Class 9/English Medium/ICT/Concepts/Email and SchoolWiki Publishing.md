# Email and SchoolWiki Publishing

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 60–66**

Email is electronic mail. An email address has a username and a service-provider domain; the chapter also describes it as a common digital identity and contact point for online services. SchoolWiki membership requires an email address, account details, and a CAPTCHA check before email verification.

The chapter teaches SchoolWiki editing in **Ezhuthukalari**, a practice area where each learner can make a training page. Learners add text, apply bold/italic formatting, choose headings, insert an image, save changes, and review the page. The book advises practising before contributing edits to public wiki pages and adding information carefully.

**Connection:** a design project can continue beyond its image file: the GIMP poster from [[../Chapters/01 - Poster Images]] can become an image resource in a wiki page.
