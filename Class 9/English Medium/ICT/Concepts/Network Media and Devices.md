# Network Media and Devices

Computers exchange data over wired or wireless media. The chapter describes optical fibre carrying data as light with little transmission loss, plastic-coated copper carrying analogue signals over longer distances, and wireless links using radio waves and satellites (printed pp. 61–62). A media converter changes signals between transmission media and digital form usable by a computer (p. 62).

For local wired links, **UTP** (Unshielded Twisted Pair) cable bundles four twisted wire pairs. A connector jack at the cable end is called **RJ45**; the computer’s port receives it (pp. 63–64). The network diagram shows computers and peripherals linked through switches, a Wi-Fi router, and a media converter (p. 65).

A hub sends transmitted data to all connected computers; a switch sends it to the intended device, reducing traffic and improving communication speed. Switches are described as more commonly used today (p. 65).
