# Online Transactions and Two-factor Authentication

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 69–71**

Online transactions include buying or selling goods and services, transferring money, online banking, and paying bills (Table 5.2, p. 70). Because these services involve accounts and money, the chapter highlights safe access practices.

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)** adds a second verification step to a password. In the textbook’s example, after entering a password, the user supplies a one-time code (OTP) delivered to a phone or email address.

The chapter also advises checking whether online offers are genuine and avoiding suspicious links (p. 72).
