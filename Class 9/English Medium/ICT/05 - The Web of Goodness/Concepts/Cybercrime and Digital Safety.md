# Cybercrime and Digital Safety

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 73–75**

The chapter describes several risks in cyberspace:

- **Malware:** harmful software that can enter through email, downloads, or removable drives, then leak information or cause damage.
- **Phishing:** deceptive emails or messages that use false prizes, jobs, or awards to trick people into revealing information.
- **Cyberstalking:** harassment that gathers information about a person and uses threats or blackmail.
- **Camera hacking:** unauthorized access to a device camera to steal private images or video.
- **Crypto hacking:** theft of digital currencies from accounts, often aided by phishing or malware.
- **IoT attacks:** intrusion into connected-device networks to steal data, disrupt systems, or misuse devices.

The illustrated precautions (Figure 5.15, p. 75) include using secure passwords, sharing personal details only with credible services, keeping systems updated, avoiding public Wi-Fi for financial transactions, reporting suspicious activity to authorities, and staying informed about emerging threats. The chapter also urges fact-checking information before sharing it.
