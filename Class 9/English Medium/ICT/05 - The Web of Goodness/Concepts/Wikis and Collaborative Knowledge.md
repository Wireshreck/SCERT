# Wikis and Collaborative Knowledge

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 58–60**

A wiki is a collaboratively edited website. The textbook traces “wiki” to the Hawaiian “WikiWiki,” meaning “quick,” and identifies Ward Cunningham’s 1995 WikiWikiWeb as the first wiki. Wikipedia is presented as a global knowledge repository whose contributors can edit pages and whose volunteers monitor changes.

SchoolWiki applies collaborative publishing to Kerala schools. Built with MediaWiki, it collects school histories, student creative work, teacher learning resources, arts-festival materials, and digital magazines. The chapter emphasizes adding accurate, useful material and correcting errors responsibly.
