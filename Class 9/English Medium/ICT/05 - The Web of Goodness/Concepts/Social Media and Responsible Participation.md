# Social Media and Responsible Participation

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 5 · printed pp. 67–69**

The chapter organizes social-media uses as resource exchange, networking, building social groups, real-time communication, and e-commerce (Figures 5.12 and Table 5.1, pp. 67–68). These platforms support sharing and connection, but participation should be thoughtful.

Its guidance is to check privacy settings, limit personal details such as addresses and phone numbers, report disrespectful behaviour, avoid spreading misinformation, be cautious with online strangers, take screen breaks, respect others’ privacy, and talk with trusted adults about harmful experiences.
