# Slide Design, Animation and Media

The chapter recommends preparing information, images, and videos before creating slides. Its example starts with a symbolic image, then adds a title and animates text. Impress provides slide layouts, animation categories/effects, drawing shapes, image ordering, and fields such as slide number (printed pp. 35–37, 40–41).

Good slide design depends on imagination and colour awareness, but should preserve readability: consistent themes, complementary colours, effective blank space, clear content hierarchy, and minimal unnecessary embellishment (p. 40). Later slides should continue the visual system established by the master and title slide (pp. 41–42).
