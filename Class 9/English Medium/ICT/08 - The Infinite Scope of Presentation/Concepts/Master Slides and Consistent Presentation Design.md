# Master Slides and Consistent Presentation Design

A master slide is a reusable template that provides a common basis for the slides in a presentation. In Impress, the chapter opens Master View, chooses a layout, and formats text levels, font, size, colour, shapes, and fields such as slide number (printed pp. 37–41). Closing Master View returns to normal editing; a prepared master can be applied to new slides. More than one master may be created, renamed, or removed (pp. 41–42).

Using a master makes design changes consistent across slides. A coherent colour scheme, proportional content arrangement, and readable spacing help a deck feel unified (pp. 41–42).
