# Chapter 8 — The Infinite Scope of Presentation

**Standard IX · English Medium · ICT · printed pp. 35–48**

Using a presentation on “Bad habits and Heart Health” as its running example, the chapter develops a LibreOffice Impress deck from a storyboard and opening image through text, animation, master slides, shapes, slide numbering, chart and infographic design, and presentation delivery. Screenshots show Impress controls and successive stages of slide creation; the heart-risk chart and infographic compare the same data in different forms.

## Topics and concepts

- [[Concepts/Master Slides and Consistent Presentation Design]] — create and format reusable master layouts, then apply them to other slides (pp. 37–42).
- [[Concepts/Slide Design, Animation and Media]] — build visual slides with titles, images, text, shapes, animation, and numbering (pp. 35–37, 40–41).
- [[Concepts/Charts and Infographics]] — turn comparative data into charts and concise infographics (pp. 42–45).
- [[Concepts/Presentation Skills and Online Tools]] — communication practice, collaborative platforms, online presentation venues, and AI-generated decks (pp. 35–36, 45–47).

## Activities and assessment

Learners prepare the heart-health presentation, design a master, format slide text, add shapes and numbers, create later slides with consistent style, use a chart/infographic, and present to classmates. The assessment checks master-slide behavior and purpose; follow-up tasks are a rain-conservation master slide and an infographic comparing internet penetration in four regions (printed pp. 47–48).
