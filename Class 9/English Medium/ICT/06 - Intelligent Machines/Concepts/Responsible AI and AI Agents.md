# Responsible AI and AI Agents

The chapter says AI output may be inaccurate or shaped by the interests of its creators, so learners should verify it. Its responsible-use principles are: **avoid harm** and misleading content; **respect privacy**; **be honest** and credit AI assistance; and **think critically** by reviewing and checking generated information (printed pp. 17–18).

An **AI agent** is introduced by analogy with a travel agent that coordinates multiple tasks. The example prompt asks an AI agent to arrange a trip, potentially coordinating transport, accommodation, food, tickets, and payment (p. 18). The example highlights delegation across services and the chapter notes that long-term effects of AI remain difficult to predict.
