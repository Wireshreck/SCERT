# AI Image Generation

AI systems can create images from written prompts. The textbook activity asks for an image of an Indian girl planting a tree for an Environment Day poster; it then gives an imaginative example of a cat selling bangles (printed pp. 14–16). If the generated result does not match the request, the learner is asked to adjust the prompt and try again (p. 16).

The activity positions AI as an aid to creative work. A sidebar reminds learners to continue practising their own writing, drawing, music, and other skills rather than letting tool use hinder their development (p. 16).
