# ICT — Subject Overview

**Standard IX · English Medium · First Edition 2025**

The current Samagra English Medium listing provides a 2025 Part I and Part II pair. The Part I textbook develops practical digital creation and related ICT skills through five chapters:

1. [[01 - Poster Images/01 - Poster Images]] — create a promotional poster in GIMP using layers, image selection and editing, text, effects, shapes, and export (printed pp. 7–22).
2. [[02 - Styles to Get it All in Style/02 - Styles to Get it All in Style]] — optical character recognition, Writer styles, footnotes/endnotes, and an automatic table of contents (printed pp. 24–36).
3. [[03 - Let’s Develop Software/03 - Let’s Develop Software]] — PictoBlox interface design, sprites, time values, variables, alarm conditions, sound, and a Python animation (printed pp. 37–47).
4. [[04 - Data Analysis Made Easy/04 - Data Analysis Made Easy]] — density formulas, LOOKUP-based categories, COUNTIF summaries, AutoFilter, and sheet protection (printed pp. 48–57).
5. [[05 - The Web of Goodness/05 - The Web of Goodness]] — collaborative wikis, SchoolWiki, social media, online services, SAMAGRA Plus resources, and cybersecurity (printed pp. 58–76).
