# Footnotes and Endnotes

**Standard IX · ICT · Chapter 2 · printed pp. 32–33**

A footnote supplies an explanation, hint, or reference at the bottom of the page where its marker appears. An endnote places that supporting information at the end of the document. Writer numbers these notes and links the marker near the term to its note, helping readers move between them.

In the textbook workflow, place the cursor after the word, choose the Footnote command, and type the explanation in the note area. Choose Endnote when the notes should be collected at the end instead. The chapter’s folklore encyclopedia activity uses supplied terms and explanations to practise both forms.
