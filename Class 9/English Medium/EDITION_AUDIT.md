## Verification method

Current Samagra PDFs were selected from the Class 9 English-medium portal and verified against the preserved local files. SHA-256 comparison matched for Biology, Chemistry, English, ICT 2025, Mathematics, Physical Education, Physics, and both Social Science subjects. Art Education and Work Integrated Education were downloaded directly from their visible Samagra links. Covers show 2024 for the revised textbooks/activity books and 2025 for ICT. The ICT Part I 2019 file remains as a separate legacy source; the current curriculum uses the 2025 Part I PDF.
