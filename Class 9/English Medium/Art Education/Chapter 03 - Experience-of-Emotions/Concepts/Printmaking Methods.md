# Printmaking Methods

Uses colour, songs, dance, film and masks to explore how artistic choices express emotion and social experience. Examples include contemporary dance, camera framing and performance traditions; activities ask students to interpret and create. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 41–61.
