# Script, Storyboard and Production

Applies music, design, dance choreography, picture editing and scene awareness to practical projects. Orchestra and stage tasks connect creative ideas with arrangement, materials, movement and audience. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.
