# Window of Creativity

Brings together sound, society, sight, ideas and the silver screen. Learners consider sound waves and recording, artistic responses to society, and the stages of making a film from idea and story through shooting and editing.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 94–110.
