# Dance, Abhinaya and Choreography

Brings together sound, society, sight, ideas and the silver screen. Learners consider sound waves and recording, artistic responses to society, and the stages of making a film from idea and story through shooting and editing. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 94–110.
