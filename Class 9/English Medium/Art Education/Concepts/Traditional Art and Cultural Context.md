# Traditional Art and Cultural Context

Connects local art and life through Warli and other traditional painting, Carnatic music and performance forms. Images and listening/singing activities invite observation of visual style, rhythm, instruments and cultural setting. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 7–28.
