# Music Forms and Performance

Introduces printmaking and the preparation of cinema through scripts, storyboards and production roles. Learners compare printing methods such as relief/woodcut, lithography, screen and digital printing, then plan scenes and presentations. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 29–40.
