# Emotion, Colour and Expression

Focuses on artistic work in public and performance spaces: sculpture, public art, abhinaya sangeetham, staging and transformation through acting. Photographs and biographies provide models for designing, presenting and reflecting on work. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Art Education Standard IX Activity Book, printed pp. 80–93.
