# Carbon Allotropes

Allotropes are different forms of the same element with different physical properties. Carbon’s crystalline allotropes include diamond, graphite, graphene and fullerene; amorphous forms include charcoal, coke, lampblack and coal. Diamond’s four covalent bonds per atom explain hardness and lack of electrical conduction; graphite’s layered structure and free electrons explain lubrication and conductivity; graphene is a single graphite layer with high electrical/thermal conductivity; fullerenes include hollow buckyballs and nanotube-like buckytubes.
