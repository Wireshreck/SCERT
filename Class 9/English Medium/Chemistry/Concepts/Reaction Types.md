# Reaction Types

The unit groups reactions by observable transformation: combination forms one compound from simpler reactants; decomposition breaks a compound into two or more substances; double decomposition exchanges ions between two compounds; displacement occurs when an element in a compound is replaced by another element. Examples include CaO + H₂O, thermal decomposition, NaCl + AgNO₃ precipitation, acid-base neutralisation, and Zn + HCl. Equations and precipitate/gas illustrations distinguish the types.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 97-102.
