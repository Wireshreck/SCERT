# Groups, Periods and Periodic Families

For main-group elements, shell count gives period number. In groups 1 and 2 the number of outer electrons gives the group; for groups 13–18, add 10 to the outer-electron count. Families named in the text include alkali metals (1), alkaline earth metals (2), transition elements (3–12), and groups 13–18 families through noble gases. Noble gases have stable outer configurations (helium duplet; others octet) and are generally unreactive.
