# True Solutions, Suspensions and Colloids

A true solution is homogeneous with very small particles that cannot be seen, filtered out or scatter a light beam. Suspension particles are larger, can settle, scatter light and can be filtered. Colloids are intermediate: particles do not settle readily and are not seen with the naked eye, but scatter light (Tyndall effect). The text gives approximate size bands and introduces dispersed phase/dispersion medium and colloid classes such as sol, gel, emulsion, foam and aerosols.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 123-126.
