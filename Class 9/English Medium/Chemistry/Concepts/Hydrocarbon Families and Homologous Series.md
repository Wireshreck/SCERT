# Hydrocarbon Families and Homologous Series

Hydrocarbons contain only carbon and hydrogen. Alkanes have only single C–C bonds and are saturated (general formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ for open chains); alkenes contain at least one double bond (CₙH₂ₙ); alkynes contain at least one triple bond (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂). Successive members of a homologous series differ by –CH₂–, share a general formula and similar chemical properties, and show gradual physical-property changes.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 154-161.
