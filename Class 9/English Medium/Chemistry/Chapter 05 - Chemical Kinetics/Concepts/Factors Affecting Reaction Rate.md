# Factors Affecting Reaction Rate

Rate depends on reactant nature, concentration, exposed surface area of solids, temperature and catalysts. The textbook’s experiments compare Cu/Zn/Mg with acid, concentrated/dilute acid, marble chips/powder, heating, and MnO₂-catalysed hydrogen peroxide. Increasing available particles or their effective collision frequency raises rate; temperature increases the fraction reaching threshold energy. A catalyst speeds the reaction without permanent chemical change to itself.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 102-109.
