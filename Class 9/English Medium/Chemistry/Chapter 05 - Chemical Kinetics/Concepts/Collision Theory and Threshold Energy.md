# Collision Theory and Threshold Energy

The textbook explains reactions through collisions between reactant particles. Only effective collisions lead to products; particles need a sufficient excess amount of energy, called threshold energy. Higher particle concentration and temperature increase effective collisions and reaction rate. The model is used qualitatively to interpret the textbook’s demonstrations.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 103-108.
