# Atomic Number, Mass Number and Isotopes

Atomic number Z is the proton count; for a neutral atom it also equals electron count. Mass number A = protons + neutrons, so neutrons = A − Z. Isotopes are atoms of one element with the same Z and different A. The textbook depicts protium, deuterium and tritium; it notes carbon isotopes 12, 13 and 14, including radiocarbon dating and a stated use of carbon-13 in metabolic studies. Isotopes retain similar chemical properties but may differ physically.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 18-20.
