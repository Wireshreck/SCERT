# Shells and Electron Configuration

Electrons occupy shells (K, L, M, N); the introductory capacity relation is 2n². The text says lower energy shells fill before higher ones and presents configurations through atomic number 18, with an outer-shell octet convention. Examples include Al 2,8,3 and Ar 2,8,8. Orbit diagrams represent nucleus protons/neutrons and shell electrons; they connect atomic number, neutral-atom electron count and configuration.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 15-18.
