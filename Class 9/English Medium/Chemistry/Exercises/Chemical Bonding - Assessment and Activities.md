# Chemical Bonding - Assessment and Activities

Exercises ask for Lewis diagrams, ionic/covalent classifications, valencies and formulae for salts, acids and bases, electron transfer in ionic compounds, shared-electron counts and bond order. Extended tasks include magnesium nitride, hydrocarbon bonding and a conductivity comparison using salt and glucose solutions.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 65-68.
