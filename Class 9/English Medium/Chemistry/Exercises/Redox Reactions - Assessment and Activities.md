# Redox Reactions - Assessment and Activities

Assessment covers balancing equations, mass calculations, oxidation-number assignment, identifying oxidised/reduced species and agents, and distinguishing redox from non-redox reactions. Extended investigations include iron/sulphur, calcium carbide, aluminium/iodine and industrial importance of redox. No answer key is provided here.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 84-86.
