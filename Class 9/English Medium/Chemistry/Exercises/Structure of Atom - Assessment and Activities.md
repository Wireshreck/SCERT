# Structure of Atom - Assessment and Activities

The assessment asks students to infer cathode-ray properties, derive proton/neutron/electron counts from atomic and mass numbers, draw shell configurations, identify isotope/isobar pairs and match historical models/scientists. Extended activities ask for scientist biographies, a discovery timeline, radioisotope uses and interview questions for Rutherford.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 21-22.
