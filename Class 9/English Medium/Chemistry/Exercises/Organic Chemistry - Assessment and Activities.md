# Organic Chemistry - Assessment and Activities

Assessment asks for structural/condensed/molecular formulae, family/general formula, naming, cyclic versus open-chain structures, petroleum separation and global warming. Extended tasks build ball-and-stick models, discuss climate change and organic chemistry, and construct six-carbon cyclic models. No textbook answer key is supplied in these notes.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 171-174.
