# Chemical Kinetics - Assessment and Activities

Assessment classifies equations by reaction type and balances products, and asks how concentration, surface area, temperature and catalysts affect rate. Extended experiments measure hydrogen volume over time for magnesium with dilute/concentrated acid and varied temperature/surface area, and investigate potassium persulphate/iodide and hydrogen-iodine reactions. The map records tasks, not unprovided answer keys.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 110-112.
