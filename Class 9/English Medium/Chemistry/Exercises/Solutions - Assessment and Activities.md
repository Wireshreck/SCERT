# Solutions - Assessment and Activities

Exercises cover solute/solvent, mass percentage, saturated/unsaturated conversion, reading solubility data and gas-solubility graphs, and classifying household mixtures as true solutions, suspensions or colloids. Extended work includes crystal growth and food safety/mixture investigations.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 130-132.
