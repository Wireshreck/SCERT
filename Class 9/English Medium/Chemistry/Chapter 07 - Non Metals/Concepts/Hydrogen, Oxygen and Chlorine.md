# Hydrogen, Oxygen and Chlorine

The chapter pairs each gas with laboratory preparation, properties, reactions and uses. Hydrogen is formed by Zn + dilute HCl and burns with a pop; oxygen can be released by heating KMnO₄ or water electrolysis and supports combustion; chlorine is prepared using KMnO₄ and concentrated HCl, dried and collected after washing. Moist chlorine bleaches via hypochlorous acid/nascent oxygen; chlorine has water-treatment and disinfectant uses. The pages include apparatus drawings and identification tests.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 134-150.
