# Ozone and Ozone Depletion

Ozone (O₃) forms when ultraviolet radiation splits O₂ and oxygen atoms recombine with O₂. Ozone absorbs ultraviolet radiation and cycles back to oxygen. The textbook explains that CFC-derived chlorine disrupts this cycle and depletes the ozone layer; it names alternative refrigerant categories and references International Ozone Day.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 146-147.
