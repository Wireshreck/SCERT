# Nitrogen Cycle and Fertilizers

Atmospheric nitrogen is relatively inert; lightning can form nitrogen oxides that lead to soil nitrates, and Rhizobium bacteria in legume roots fix nitrogen into usable compounds. Plants also receive nutrients from organic or chemical fertilizers. The table compares organic fertilizers (soil-friendly, slower nutrient release) and chemical fertilizers (quick N/P/K supply, but possible soil-structure, acidity and pollution impacts); the book recommends judicious scientific use.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 143-145.
