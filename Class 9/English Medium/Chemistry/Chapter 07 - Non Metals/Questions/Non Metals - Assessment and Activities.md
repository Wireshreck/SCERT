# Non Metals - Assessment and Activities

Exercises identify gases from preparation/use clues, compare carbon allotropes, test chloride salts, explain hydrogen fuel and chlorine bleaching, and interpret carbon dioxide preparation/identification. Extended activities include a chlorine bleaching note, gas-use table and fertilizer-factory report.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 150-152.
