# Chemical Formula Charge Balance

For an electrically neutral ionic formula, choose the smallest whole-number ratio of ions for which total positive charge + total negative charge = 0. The text demonstrates valency interchange followed by simplification, e.g. Ca²⁺ and SO₄²⁻ give CaSO₄.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 60-64.
