# Conservation in Balanced Equations

For each element, atom count on reactant side = atom count on product side; coefficients may change, formula subscripts may not. Example: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 71-74.
