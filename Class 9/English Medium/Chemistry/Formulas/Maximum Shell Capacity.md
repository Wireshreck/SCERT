# Maximum Shell Capacity

The introductory shell-capacity relation is 2n² electrons for shell number n (K=1, L=2, M=3, N=4). The textbook also emphasizes filling lower energy shells first.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 16.
