# Chapter 08 - Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry studies carbon compounds. The unit develops carbon’s valency and catenation through structural, condensed and molecular formulae. Hydrocarbons contain only carbon and hydrogen. Open-chain hydrocarbons are classified by bond type: alkanes (single bonds, saturated), alkenes (at least one double bond) and alkynes (at least one triple bond), each with general formulae and homologous-series patterns. IUPAC naming uses carbon-count word roots and suffixes -ane, -ene and -yne. Cyclic hydrocarbons are divided into alicyclic and aromatic; examples include cycloalkanes, benzene and naphthalene. The chapter surveys petroleum fractional distillation products and uses, coal formation/types and natural gas, then connects greenhouse gases and the greenhouse effect to global warming. Structural diagrams and ball-and-stick models support exercises on formulae, names, chain/ring isomers and fuels.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 153-174.
