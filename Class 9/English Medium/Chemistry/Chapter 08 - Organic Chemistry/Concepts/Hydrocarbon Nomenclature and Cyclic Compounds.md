# Hydrocarbon Nomenclature and Cyclic Compounds

The unit builds IUPAC names from carbon-count roots meth-, eth-, prop-, but-, pent- and onward, plus -ane/-ene/-yne according to carbon-carbon bond type. Cyclic hydrocarbons include alicyclic rings (named with cyclo-) and aromatic compounds such as benzene and naphthalene. Structural diagrams teach students to infer molecular/condensed formulae and compare open-chain and ring structures.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 161-166.
