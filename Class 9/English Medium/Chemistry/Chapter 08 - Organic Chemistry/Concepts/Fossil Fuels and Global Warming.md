# Fossil Fuels and Global Warming

Petroleum is a fossil-fuel hydrocarbon mixture separated by fractional distillation into gases, petrol, naphtha, kerosene, diesel, lubricating oil, fuel oil, wax and bitumen, each with uses. Coal forms by carbonisation of buried plant remains; natural gas is mainly methane. Combustion and other human activity increase greenhouse gases; these trap some outgoing infrared heat (greenhouse effect), contributing to global warming. The textbook presents a table/graph of gas contributions and asks students to consider consequences and remedies.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 167-170.
