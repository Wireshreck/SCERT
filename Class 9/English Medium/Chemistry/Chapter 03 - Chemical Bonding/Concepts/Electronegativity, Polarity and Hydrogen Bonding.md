# Electronegativity, Polarity and Hydrogen Bonding

Electronegativity is an atom’s relative ability to attract a shared electron pair. Unequal attraction can create partial charges and a polar covalent molecule (the H–Cl diagram shows δ+ and δ−); the text uses electronegativity difference as a guide to ionic versus covalent character and cautions that molecular geometry also affects polarity. Hydrogen bonding is described as an intermolecular attraction involving partially positive hydrogen bonded to a more electronegative atom, used to explain notable properties of water.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 55-58.
