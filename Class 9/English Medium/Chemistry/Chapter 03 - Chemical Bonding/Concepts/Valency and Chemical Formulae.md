# Valency and Chemical Formulae

Valency is the number of electrons an atom loses, gains or shares in a reaction. Formula writing combines symbols and uses valencies/ionic charges as subscripts, then simplifies to the smallest whole-number ratio; a subscript 1 is omitted. The unit extends the method to acids (formula determined by the charge of the negative ion and acid basicity), hydroxide bases, and salts formed from positive and negative ions. Salts are electrically neutral: the total positive and negative charge sums to zero.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 58-64.
