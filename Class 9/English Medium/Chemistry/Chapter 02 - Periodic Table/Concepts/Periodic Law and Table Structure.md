# Periodic Law and Table Structure

The modern periodic law states that chemical and physical properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers. The table organizes 118 elements in 7 horizontal periods and 18 vertical groups; elements in a group generally share outer-electron patterns and chemical properties. Mendeleev’s mass-based table had limitations for isotopes and mass-order anomalies; Moseley’s atomic-number basis addresses those cases. Table reading activities identify element name, symbol, atomic number and electron configuration.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 24-30.
