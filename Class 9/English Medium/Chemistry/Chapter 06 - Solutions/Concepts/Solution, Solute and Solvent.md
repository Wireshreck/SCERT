# Solution, Solute and Solvent

A solution is a homogeneous mixture with the same property throughout. The solute is the substance dissolved; the solvent is the substance that dissolves it. The state of a solution is usually the state of the solvent. Examples include salt water, brass (Zn in Cu), soda water, alcohol-water and atmospheric air. In an aqueous solution, water is treated as the solvent.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 114-117.
