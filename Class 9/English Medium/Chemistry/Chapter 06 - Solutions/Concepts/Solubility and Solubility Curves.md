# Solubility and Solubility Curves

Solubility is the mass in grams of solute required to saturate 100 g of a solvent at a given temperature. Curves compare salts such as KNO₃, NaCl and CaSO₄: solubility often increases with temperature, but each substance behaves differently. The book highlights Glauber’s salt as anomalous, with a maximum around 32.4°C, and notes gas solubility rises with pressure (carbon dioxide in soda water) while generally falling as temperature rises.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 121-122.
