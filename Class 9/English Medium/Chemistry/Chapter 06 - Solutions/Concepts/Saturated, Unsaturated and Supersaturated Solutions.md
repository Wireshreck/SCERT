# Saturated, Unsaturated and Supersaturated Solutions

At a particular temperature, a saturated solution contains the maximum amount of solute that dissolves; an unsaturated solution contains less and can dissolve more. Heating can dissolve additional solute; cooling a carefully prepared supersaturated solution may cause excess solute to crystallise. The chapter uses ammonium chloride and seed crystals of copper sulphate/potassium nitrate to demonstrate these states and crystallisation.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 118-120.
