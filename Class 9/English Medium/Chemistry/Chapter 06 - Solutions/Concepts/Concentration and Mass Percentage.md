# Concentration and Mass Percentage

Concentration describes the amount of solute dissolved in a fixed amount of solvent. A dilute solution has relatively little solute; a concentrated one has more. The unit introduces mass percentage as grams of solute in 100 g of solution and shows an example with 10 g salt plus 90 g water. It also mentions ppm, volume percentage, molarity and molality without developing them here.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 117-118.
