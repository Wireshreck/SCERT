# Oxidation Number

For ionic compounds, the charge on each ion is its oxidation number. For covalent compounds, the text assigns shared electrons to the more electronegative atom. The sum of oxidation numbers in a compound is zero; in an elemental molecule, it is zero.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 79-80.
