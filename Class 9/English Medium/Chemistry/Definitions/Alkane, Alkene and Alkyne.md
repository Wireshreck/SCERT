# Alkane, Alkene and Alkyne

Open-chain hydrocarbons with only single carbon-carbon bonds are alkanes; those with at least one C=C bond are alkenes; those with at least one C≡C bond are alkynes.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 155-159.
