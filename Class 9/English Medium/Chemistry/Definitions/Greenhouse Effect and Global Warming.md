# Greenhouse Effect and Global Warming

The greenhouse effect is trapping and re-radiation of some outgoing infrared heat by atmospheric greenhouse gases. Global warming is the increase in average temperature of Earth and its atmosphere.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 169.
