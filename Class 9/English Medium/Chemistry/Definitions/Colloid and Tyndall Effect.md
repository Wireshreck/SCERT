# Colloid and Tyndall Effect

A colloidal mixture lies between a true solution and suspension; its dispersed particles scatter a beam of light. This scattering is the Tyndall effect.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 125-126.
