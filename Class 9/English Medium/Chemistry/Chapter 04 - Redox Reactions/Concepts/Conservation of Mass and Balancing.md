# Conservation of Mass and Balancing

The chapter states the law of conservation of mass: total mass of reactants equals total mass of products in a chemical reaction. A balanced equation has equal numbers of atoms of every element on both sides. Balance by adjusting coefficients only; preserve chemical formula subscripts. Worked examples include 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO, Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂, and 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 70-75.
