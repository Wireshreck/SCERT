# Oxidation, Reduction and Agents

Oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain. In a redox reaction both happen simultaneously. An oxidising agent causes another species to oxidise and itself gets reduced; a reducing agent causes another species to reduce and itself gets oxidised. The examples include Na + Cl₂, Mg + O₂, H₂ + Cl₂ and Fe + S. Oxidation-number increase indicates oxidation, decrease indicates reduction.

**Textbook reference:** Chemistry Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 75-83.
