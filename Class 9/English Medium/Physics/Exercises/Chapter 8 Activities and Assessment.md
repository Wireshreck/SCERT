# Chapter 8 Activities and Assessment

Activities identify vibrating sources and instrument parts, test sound through solids/liquids/gases, compare speeds, and connect sound to hearing. Assessment asks about medium, vibration, vacuum, instrument vibration, sound speed, hearing and noise. Extended work includes sound-making toys, string telephones and public awareness of noise pollution.
