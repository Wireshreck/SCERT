# Chapter 4 Activities and Assessment

The chapter includes spring-balance comparisons, mass/distance tables, universal-gravitation calculations, feather-and-coin free-fall demonstrations, mass/weight comparisons, circular-motion and satellite reasoning. Assessment asks for Earth/Moon and other-planet weight, free-fall timing, acceleration, and circular-motion identification. Extended activity asks learners to model the Solar System and compare weights across planets.
