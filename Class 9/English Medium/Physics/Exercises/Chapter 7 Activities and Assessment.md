# Chapter 7 Activities and Assessment

Circuit activities construct low-voltage LED circuits, measure cells and potential differences, test Ohm’s law, compare conductor materials/dimensions, use a rheostat, and build series/parallel resistor networks. Assessment reads circuit diagrams and graphs, calculates current/voltage/resistance, compares networks, and checks meter connections. Extended activities include circuit construction and cell testing. The chapter warns against household mains in classroom experiments.
