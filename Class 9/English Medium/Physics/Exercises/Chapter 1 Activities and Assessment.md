# Chapter 1 Activities and Assessment

The chapter asks learners to trace light through water, smoke and glass; compare rays at normal and oblique incidence; order media by optical density; calculate refractive index and light speed; identify critical-angle and total-internal-reflection cases from diagrams; and explain mirage, twinkling, periscopes, reflectors and optical fibres. Extended activities include prism periscopes and testing critical angles. This note records task types and does not supply unverified answers.
