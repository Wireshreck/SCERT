# Chapter 5 Activities and Assessment

Experiments compare apparent weight in air and liquids, vary fluid density and immersed volume, measure displaced water, and test floating shapes. Assessment covers buoyant force, Archimedes’ principle, relative density and flotation; extended work builds and compares hydrometers/lactometers. No answer key is supplied in the textbook pages reviewed.
