# Refractive Index

For a medium relative to vacuum, the refractive index is the ratio of light speed in vacuum to its speed in the medium:

`n = c / v`

`c` is the speed of light in vacuum (`3×10⁸ m/s` in the chapter); `v` is speed in the medium. The ratio is dimensionless. Thus `v=c/n`; a larger refractive index corresponds to lower light speed in that medium. Use consistent units. Example from the chapter: a medium where light travels at `2×10⁸ m/s` has `n=1.5`.
