# Buoyancy and Relative Density

- Buoyant force from apparent-weight loss: `F_b = W_air − W_fluid`.
- Archimedes’ principle: `F_b = weight of displaced fluid`.
- Relative density: `ρ_substance / ρ_water` (dimensionless).

Use newtons for force/weight and consistent density units in the ratio. The chapter uses water density approximately `1000 kg/m³`; relative density compares a substance with water. For a floating object, its weight equals the weight of displaced fluid. See [[../Concepts/Buoyancy and Archimedes Principle]].
