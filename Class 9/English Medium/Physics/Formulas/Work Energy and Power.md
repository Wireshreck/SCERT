# Work, Energy and Power

- Work for force parallel to displacement: `W = F s`.
- Gravitational potential energy near Earth: `Eₚ = mgh`.
- Kinetic energy: `Eₖ = ½mv²`.
- Power: `P = W/t`.

Use SI units: force N, displacement m, work/energy J, time s, power W. The work equation here assumes force along the displacement; opposing force gives negative work. The potential-energy expression uses a chosen reference level and approximately constant `g`. See [[../06 - Work and Energy/Concepts/Work Energy and Power]].
