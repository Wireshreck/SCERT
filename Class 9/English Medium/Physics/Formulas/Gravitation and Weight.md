# Gravitation and Weight

- Universal gravitation: `F = Gm₁m₂/r²`
- Acceleration due to gravity at radius `R`: `g = GM/R²`
- Weight: `W = mg`

`m₁`, `m₂` are masses; `r` is separation between centres; `M` is the attracting body’s mass; `G=6.67×10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²`. Near Earth, this edition uses `g≈9.8 m/s²`. Weight is measured in newtons and changes when `g` changes; mass is measured in kilograms and does not. Apply free-fall motion equations with gravitational acceleration when acceleration can be treated constant over the relevant height.
