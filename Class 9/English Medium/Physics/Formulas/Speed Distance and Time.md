# Speed, Distance and Time for Sound

For the chapter’s uniform propagation example: `distance = speed × time`. Rearranged, `time = distance/speed`. Keep units consistent; e.g., speed in metres per second and time in seconds gives distance in metres. The textbook example uses `350 m/s × 3 s = 1050 m` for sound travelling from a firecracker. The speed depends on the medium and temperature; do not apply one value to every medium.
