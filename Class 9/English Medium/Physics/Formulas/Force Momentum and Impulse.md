# Force, Momentum and Impulse

- Momentum: `p = mv`
- Rate of change of momentum: `(mv − mu)/t`
- For constant mass: `F = ma`
- Impulse for a constant force: `J = FΔt = Δp`

`m` is mass, `u` and `v` initial/final velocities, and `t` the interval. SI units: momentum `kg·m/s`, force `N = kg·m/s²`, impulse `N·s`. Direction/sign matters: a force opposite motion can be negative under the chosen convention. The impulse relation explains why increasing collision time lowers average force for a given momentum change.
