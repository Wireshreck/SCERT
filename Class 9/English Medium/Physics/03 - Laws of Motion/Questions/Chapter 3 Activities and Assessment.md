# Chapter 3 Activities and Assessment

Activities use force diagrams, pulleys and carts, Galileo’s channel, inertia demonstrations, Newton’s cradle and spring balances. Assessment covers resultant forces, momentum and rate of momentum change, `F=ma`, impulse, stopping time, and action–reaction examples. Extended activities connect force and impulse to road safety, shock absorbers and everyday applications.
