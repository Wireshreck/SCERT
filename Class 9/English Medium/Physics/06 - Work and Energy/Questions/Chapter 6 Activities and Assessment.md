# Chapter 6 Activities and Assessment

Tasks classify work/no-work situations, calculate work and energy, compare potential and kinetic energy, trace energy conversions, interpret a force–displacement graph, and calculate power. The assessment includes lifting, energy conservation, power, graphs and mechanical-energy changes. Extended activities explore sports equipment, household energy use and vehicle safety.
