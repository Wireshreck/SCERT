# Physics — Part 1

**Standard IX · English Medium · First Edition 2024**

This volume develops geometrical optics and mechanics through experiments, ray diagrams, tables, motion graphs, numerical problems and road-safety applications. Its 88 PDF pages have been visually reviewed, including the four chapters, all figures/tables/assessments, end activities, and civic inserts.

## Chapters

1. [[Chapters/01 - Refraction of Light]] — optical density, refraction, refractive index, total internal reflection and applications (printed pp. 7–26).
2. [[Chapters/02 - Equations of Motion]] — distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs and constant-acceleration equations (pp. 27–52).
3. [[Chapters/03 - Laws of Motion]] — resultant forces, inertia, momentum, Newton’s laws and impulse (pp. 53–72).
4. [[Chapters/04 - Gravitation]] — universal gravitation, g, mass and weight, free fall, circular motion (pp. 73–86).
