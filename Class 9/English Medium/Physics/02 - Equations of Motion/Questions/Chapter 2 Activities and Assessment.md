# Chapter 2 Activities and Assessment

Learners compare routes and displacement, classify scalar/vector quantities, calculate speed/velocity/acceleration, read road-safety signs and plot position–time and velocity–time graphs with chosen scales. Assessment includes graph interpretation, acceleration/deceleration, circular travel, vertical throws, unit conversions and numerical problems applying the three constant-acceleration equations. Extended work includes road-safety projects and motion investigations.
