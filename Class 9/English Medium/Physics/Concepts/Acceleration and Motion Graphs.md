# Acceleration and Motion Graphs

Acceleration is change in velocity per unit time; it is a vector. If velocity decreases in the chosen positive direction, acceleration is negative (retardation). Uniform acceleration means equal velocity changes in equal time intervals; otherwise it is non-uniform. The chapter’s vehicle tables and examples distinguish speed change from direction change.

A position–time graph represents position against time; its slope corresponds to velocity. A velocity–time graph plots velocity vertically and time horizontally; slope represents acceleration and signed area under the curve represents displacement over the interval. Interpret graph scales and units before reading values. For uniform acceleration the chapter applies [[../Formulas/Equations of Uniform Motion]].
