# Newton’s Laws and Inertia

The resultant (effective) force is the vector sum of simultaneous forces. Balanced forces have zero resultant; unbalanced forces change or tend to change rest or motion. An external unbalanced force is needed to change motion. Internal forces in a system cannot change the motion of the system as a whole.

Newton’s first law states that an object remains at rest or in uniform straight-line motion unless an unbalanced external force acts. Inertia is this resistance to a change in state; inertia of rest and of motion are illustrated by passengers, coins and containers. The text relates greater mass to greater inertia. Newton’s second law relates force to rate of momentum change and yields `F=ma` for constant mass. Newton’s third law says forces occur in equal and opposite pairs on different bodies simultaneously, so they do not cancel on one body.
