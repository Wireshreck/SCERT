# Work, Energy and Power

In this textbook, work is done when a force produces displacement in its direction. Work can be positive or negative depending on the direction relation; a force perpendicular to displacement does no work in this simplified treatment. Energy is capacity to do work. Mechanical energy includes potential energy (position/configuration) and kinetic energy (motion). The chapter develops `mgh` and `½mv²`, and describes conversion between forms while total energy is conserved in the idealised examples.

Power measures work per time. For the same work, completing it in less time means greater power. SI units: work/energy joule, power watt. The hydroelectric diagram traces stored water through turbine/generator to electricity and a lit bulb. See [[../Formulas/Work Energy and Power]].
