# Let It Go

The song from Frozen presents a speaker releasing fear and constraint and claiming a more independent identity. Repetition, imagery of cold and transformation, and shifts in voice carry the emotional change. The textbook pairs the lyrics with listening, interpretation, poetic-device and media questions; it also includes language practice and a project on advertising and youth culture.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 169-180.
