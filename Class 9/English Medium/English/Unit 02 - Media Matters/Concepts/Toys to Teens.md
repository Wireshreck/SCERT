# Toys to Teens

The article examines how advertising targets children and adolescents, shapes desires and links products with popularity or identity. It considers media strategies and the influence of marketing on family choices and consumption. The illustrated examples invite learners to analyse audience, purpose, persuasive claims and visual design, then create or critique advertisements using evidence and ethical judgment.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 157-165.
