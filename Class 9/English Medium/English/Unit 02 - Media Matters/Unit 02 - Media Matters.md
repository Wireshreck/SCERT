# Unit 02 - Media Matters

This unit examines how media shape choices, identity and culture. Toys to Teens discusses advertising directed at children and the pressure to consume; The Ink and the Melody explores childhood, popular culture, music and a young person’s relationship with an iconic performer; Let It Go uses a song to discuss self-expression and emotional release. The unit also develops media literacy, privacy and cyber safety, advertisement analysis, reported speech, clauses, homophones, persuasive language and survey work.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 155-180.
