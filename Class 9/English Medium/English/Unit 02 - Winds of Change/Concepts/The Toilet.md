# The Toilet

Gita Mirchandani’s first-person narrative describes a child’s experience of inadequate toilet facilities and the family’s attempts to respond. The narrator’s embarrassment and distress reveal how sanitation affects dignity, health, education and equal participation. The story moves from a personal problem toward communication and civic responsibility; activities ask readers to consider access, voice, solutions and the story’s perspective.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 51-59.
