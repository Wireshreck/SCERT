# Unit 02 - Winds of Change

The unit follows change through social action and personal responsibility. Dreams Realised presents Gandhi’s work and ideals through narrative and biographical detail; The Toilet uses a first-person child’s account to examine sanitation, dignity and community action; Success is a poem about effort and perseverance. Activities move from comprehension to report writing, interviews, passive constructions, reported speech, adjective formation and public speaking.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 41-70.
