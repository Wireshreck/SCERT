# Unit 01 - Hours and Years

This unit considers memory, time and gratitude through a short story, a personal essay and a poem. The reading sequence pairs the child narrator’s encounter with adult life in Half a Day, a reflective account of gratitude in Debts of Gratitude, and a poem about the irreversibility of time in Nothing Twice. The page design combines illustrations with response boxes, character mapping, narrative-sequence work, vocabulary, grammar and personal writing.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 7-40.
