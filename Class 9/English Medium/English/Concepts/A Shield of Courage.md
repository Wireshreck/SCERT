# A Shield of Courage

The account profiles Neerja Bhanot’s actions as a flight attendant during the hijacking of Pan Am Flight 73 in 1986. It describes how she helped passengers under threat and died while enabling others to escape. Chronology, reporting and biographical details explain the courage and responsibility involved; the accompanying activities practise report structure, interview questions, speeches and discussion of ethical action.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 132-138.
