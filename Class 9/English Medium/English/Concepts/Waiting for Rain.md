# Waiting for Rain

Anita Nair’s story centres on a family and village during heavy monsoon rain and flooding in Kerala. The child narrator records fear, uncertainty, practical response and community care as water disrupts ordinary life. Rain is both a physical danger and an emotional presence. The textbook’s illustrations, dialogue and sensory detail support close reading; follow-up work covers point of view, sequence, vocabulary, homophones and conditional language.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 85-109.
