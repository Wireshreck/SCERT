# The Saga of the Tiffin Carriers

Hugh and Colleen Gantzer describe Mumbai’s dabbawalas: a large, coordinated service that collects, sorts, transports and returns home-cooked meals. The account explains the human organisation and delivery chain, the system’s distinctive coding and reliability, and the discipline and cooperation behind it. Photographs and diagrams clarify the route and work. Tasks include report/news writing and process explanation.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 78-84.
