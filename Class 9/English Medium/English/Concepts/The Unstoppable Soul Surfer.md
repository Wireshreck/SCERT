# The Unstoppable Soul Surfer

Bethany Hamilton’s autobiographical account describes a shark attack that cost her an arm and her determination to return to surfing. The narrative follows shock, recovery, adaptation and renewed participation, showing the role of family, faith, training and self-belief. Photographs and captions connect the prose to real events. Activities distinguish fact from reflection, develop a character profile and prepare an interview or newspaper report.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 121-131.
