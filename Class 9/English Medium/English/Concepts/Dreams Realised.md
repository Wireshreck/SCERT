# Dreams Realised

Mahadev Desai’s account presents Gandhi’s South African work and the development of satyagraha through narrative detail. The text links moral courage and non-violent resistance with organisation, self-discipline, service and social reform. Photographs and contextual notes anchor the account historically; activities practise sequencing, interpretation, report writing and public speech.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 43-50.
