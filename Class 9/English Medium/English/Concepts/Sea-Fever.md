# Sea-Fever

John Masefield’s lyric voice longs for a return to the sea. Repeated openings create the rhythm of a wish; nautical vocabulary, movement, weather and sound imagery evoke a sailor’s life. Activities identify sensory images and poetic devices and recast the poem’s journey as a choreographic or descriptive response.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 73-77.
