# Nothing Twice

Wisława Szymborska’s poem reflects on time’s irreversibility: each moment and experience occurs once, so choices and encounters cannot be exactly repeated. Repeated phrasing and contrast support a reflective tone. The accompanying activities ask learners to discuss the speaker, repetition, mood and imagery rather than treat the poem as a set of literal instructions.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 22-24.
