# Debts of Gratitude

The text reflects on acts of kindness and the lasting obligation to recognise them. Its first-person recollection links personal gratitude with memory, respect and generosity. The illustration and response questions prompt learners to distinguish the event from the narrator’s later reflection and to express gratitude in their own writing.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 17-21.
