# Half a Day

Naguib Mahfouz’s first-person story follows a child on his first school day. The initially strange school becomes a place of relationships and learning; when the narrator leaves, the world outside has changed and he discovers that a lifetime has passed. The movement from childhood to old age makes time, memory, social change and the brevity of life central. The textbook asks readers to track setting, attitude, character and narrative shifts.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 8-16.
