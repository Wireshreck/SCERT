# Success

Henry Wadsworth Longfellow’s poem considers how achievement is remembered and why effort matters. Its reflective voice invites learners to distinguish public recognition from sustained work and to notice repetition and contrast. Questions connect the poem’s idea of success with everyday experience.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 60-63.
