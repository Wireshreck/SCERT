# Unit 03 - Aspire and Explore

This unit connects exploration, courage and resilience. Sea-Fever expresses a speaker’s attraction to the sea and travel; The Saga of the Tiffin Carriers describes Mumbai’s dabbawalas and the system behind reliable delivery; Waiting for Rain presents a Kerala family’s experience of a monsoon flood. The activities inspect sound and sensory imagery, narrative viewpoint, information reporting, homophones, relative clauses, suggestions, and descriptive writing. Illustrations and diagrams support the poem, delivery system and flood narrative.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 71-109.
