# Poetic repetition and refrain

Repetition is the recurrence of words, sounds or structures for emphasis and rhythm. A refrain is a recurring line or phrase; in Walk Alone it reinforces the central call to persist.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 139-144.
