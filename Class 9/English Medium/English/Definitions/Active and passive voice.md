# Active and passive voice

In active voice, the grammatical subject performs the action; in passive voice, the subject receives it. A passive form uses an appropriate form of “be” plus a past participle; the agent may be included with “by” or omitted when unknown or unimportant.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part I, printed pp. 62-65.
