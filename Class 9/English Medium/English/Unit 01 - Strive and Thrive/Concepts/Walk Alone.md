# Walk Alone

Rabindranath Tagore’s poem urges the listener to continue a principled path when support or recognition is absent. The repeated refrain, imperative voice and images of light, sound and solitary movement create resolve. The translation is paired with activities on poetic devices, imagery, resilience and the poem’s contemporary relevance.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 139-154.
