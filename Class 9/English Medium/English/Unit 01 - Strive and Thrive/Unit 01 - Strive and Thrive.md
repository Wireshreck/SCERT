# Unit 01 - Strive and Thrive

This unit explores persistence, courage and self-reliance through a personal narrative, a report on an act of courage and a poem. The Unstoppable Soul Surfer recounts Bethany Hamilton’s return to surfing after a shark attack; A Shield of Courage profiles Neerja Bhanot and the hijacking of Pan Am Flight 73; Walk Alone presents a call to continue along a chosen path. Activities develop interview writing, report structure, character analysis, imagery and public speaking.

**Textbook reference:** English Kerala Reader Standard IX Part II, printed pp. 119-154.
