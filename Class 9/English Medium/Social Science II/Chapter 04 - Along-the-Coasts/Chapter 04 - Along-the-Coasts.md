# Along the Coasts

Compares India’s western and eastern coastal plains, regional divisions, islands, beaches, estuaries, lagoons and coastal landforms shaped by waves and tides. Maps and diagrams connect climate, soils, vegetation, resources and livelihoods with population, economic activity and hazards affecting coastal communities.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part II, printed pp. 163–182.
