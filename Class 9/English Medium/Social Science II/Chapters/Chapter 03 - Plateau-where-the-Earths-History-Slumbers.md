# Plateau where the Earth’s History Slumbers

Maps the Peninsular Plateau, including the Deccan and central/eastern highlands. It relates older geological structure to rivers, minerals, soils and vegetation, then examines farming and crops such as cotton, coffee, tea and sugarcane alongside population and urbanisation.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part I, printed pp. 53–76.
