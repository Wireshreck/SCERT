# Through the Sandy Expanse

Examines the Thar Desert’s extent, boundaries, relief, dunes and other landforms, climate, scarce rainfall, soils and vegetation. Maps and photographs support study of adaptation and livelihoods in agriculture, pastoralism, mining, handicrafts, energy and tourism, including water management and development challenges.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part II, printed pp. 143–162.
