# Price and Market

Introduces production, inputs, consumption and markets. Demand and supply schedules and curves show how quantity responds to price; their interaction establishes equilibrium. The chapter also addresses marketing, price control/support, digital marketing and sustainable production/consumption through diagrams, examples and data tasks.
