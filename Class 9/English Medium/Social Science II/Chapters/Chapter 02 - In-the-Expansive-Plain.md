# In the Expansive Plain

Explains formation of the Indo-Gangetic-Brahmaputra plain and its regional divisions, including Rajasthan, Punjab-Haryana, Ganga and Brahmaputra sections. River systems, monsoon climate, alluvial soils, vegetation, agriculture and livelihoods are interpreted through maps and regional comparisons.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part I, printed pp. 28–52.
