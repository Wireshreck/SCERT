# On the Roof of the World

Studies the Himalaya as a young mountain system shaped by tectonic movement. Maps and diagrams locate its ranges and rivers; text connects relief with climate, vegetation, soils, farming, tourism and protected areas. The chapter relates physical geography to settlement and livelihood.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part I, printed pp. 7–27.
