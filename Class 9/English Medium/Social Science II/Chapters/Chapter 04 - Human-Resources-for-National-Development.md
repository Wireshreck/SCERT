# Human Resources for National Development

Treats people and skills as resources in production and development. It introduces factors of production, productivity, barter and money/banking, then connects education, health, migration, vocational training and information access with human capital. Poverty and unemployment are considered as barriers to development.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part I, printed pp. 77–93.
