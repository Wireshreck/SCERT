# Indian Economy Through Various Sectors

Explains primary, secondary and tertiary sectors and their interdependence. National income measures, including product, income and expenditure approaches, per-capita income, GDP/GNP and value added, are supported by tables and graphs. Activities compare sectoral contributions, employment and organised/unorganised work.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part II, printed pp. 103–122.
