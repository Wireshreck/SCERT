# Regional environments of the plain

The Rajasthan, Punjab-Haryana, Ganga and Brahmaputra regions differ in rainfall, river patterns, soils and livelihoods. Maps help compare agricultural and settlement conditions.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part 1, printed pp. 37–52.
