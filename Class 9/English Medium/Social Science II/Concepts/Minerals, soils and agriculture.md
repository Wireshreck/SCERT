# Minerals, soils and agriculture

Geology supports varied mineral resources; soils and climate shape crops such as cotton, coffee, tea and sugarcane. Resource use intersects with settlement and urban growth.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science II — Part 1, printed pp. 63–76.
