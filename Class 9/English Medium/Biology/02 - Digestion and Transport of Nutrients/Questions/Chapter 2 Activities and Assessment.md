# Chapter 2 Activities and Assessment

Tasks include completing the nutrient-flow routes from villi to liver/heart, analyzing heart-chamber and valve diagrams, reconstructing the cardiac cycle, comparing pulse and heartbeat at rest and after exercise, and completing the xylem/phloem transport explanations. The final assessment asks about fat digestion, double circulation, nutrient pathways and a labelled tooth section. Extended tasks propose a heart-health awareness class and a nutritious-food event.
