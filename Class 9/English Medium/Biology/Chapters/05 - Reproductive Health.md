# Chapter 5 — Reproductive Health

## Chapter overview

The chapter frames reproductive health through care, informed choices, equality and well-being. It opens with maternal and child health contexts and addresses gender justice: equal access to education, leadership, safe mobility, fair wages and shared care work. It distinguishes biological sex characteristics from socially assigned gender roles and recognizes intersex variations and transgender identities.

The health material covers adolescent education, reproductive health, pregnancy, foetal growth by trimester, antenatal care, motherhood, breast milk and vaccination. The diagrams and explanatory passages describe reproductive organs, fertilisation and development, implantation, placenta and the protective amniotic sac. The text emphasizes nutrition and health care during pregnancy and discusses breastfeeding and colostrum as an early source of protection.

The chapter also discusses sexually transmitted infections and prevention, fertility factors, testing and vaccination, and illustrates contraception including vasectomy. The assessment asks learners to identify pregnancy stages, interpret diagrams and consider STI prevention. Treat medical schedules and statistics as the edition’s textbook content; check current clinical advice with qualified health services.

## Chapter topics

Gender justice and sex/gender; adolescence and puberty; pregnancy and fertilisation; implantation and embryo/foetal development; placenta and amniotic protection; antenatal care and nutrition; childbirth; motherhood, colostrum and breastfeeding; infant immunisation; teenage pregnancy; birth-rate variation and contraception; infertility; sexually transmitted infections; HPV and cervical cancer; reproductive-process evolution.

## Figures, tables and learning tasks

The pages use an ovulation/uterine-wall timeline, fertilisation and zygote-development diagrams, labelled foetal-protection illustration (placenta, umbilical cord, amnion and amniotic fluid), a trimester table, antenatal examination/testing panels, childbirth illustrations, and contraception and pathogen images. Tables include pregnancy food guidance and the National Immunisation Schedule activity. End assessment spans printed pp. 137–138, followed by extended activities on p. 138.
