# Respiration and Gas Exchange

Respiration releases usable energy through chemical processes in cells; it is related to, but distinct from, the movement of air during breathing. Effective gas-exchange surfaces are thin, moist, extensive and close to blood capillaries. Oxygen and carbon dioxide move between alveolar air, blood and tissues by diffusion. The chapter asks learners to explain the roles of plasma, red blood cells and haemoglobin in gas transport.

## Textbook figures and activities

The respiratory-system figure labels trachea, lungs and diaphragm. Alveolus/capillary diagrams and concentration prompts guide explanation of exchange; the assessment identifies a thick cell wall as unsuitable for an efficient exchange surface.
