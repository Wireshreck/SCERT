# Excretion and Homeostasis

Excretion removes metabolic wastes and helps maintain internal conditions. Lungs remove carbon dioxide; kidneys remove wastes in urine. If kidney function fails, haemodialysis can remove wastes across selectively permeable tubing into dialysis fluid; the illustrated process uses anticoagulant before the circuit and reverses its action before blood returns. Kidney transplantation is also discussed. The chapter’s final theme is that waste removal, transport and respiration contribute together to homeostasis.

## Textbook figures and activities

The book diagrams the respiratory and urinary systems, urine formation and a haemodialysis circuit, then asks learners to connect pulmonary/systemic circulation with gas exchange and excretion.
