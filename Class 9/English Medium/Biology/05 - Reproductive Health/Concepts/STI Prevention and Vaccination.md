# STI Prevention and Vaccination

The closing sections address sexually transmitted infections, prevention, testing, fertility factors, HPV and vaccination. The text includes a vasectomy diagram and asks about contraception and ways to reduce STI transmission. These notes describe the textbook’s educational topics; for current medical decisions, consult a qualified health professional.
