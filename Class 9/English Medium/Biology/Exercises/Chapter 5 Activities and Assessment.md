# Chapter 5 Activities and Assessment

Activities invite discussion of gender justice, maternal and child health, pregnancy care, foetal growth, breastfeeding, vaccination and STI prevention. Assessment asks about pregnancy duration, ultrasound, implantation, contraception, external fertilisation, fertility factors and trimester changes. Extended work includes adolescent-health and newborn-vaccination communication activities.
