# Chapter 3 Activities and Assessment

Tasks include completing a nephron-to-urine flowchart; interpreting urine components; performing the prescribed Benedict-reagent comparison activity and recording observation/inference; explaining the stages shown in a haemodialysis diagram; labelling the respiratory and urinary systems; and analyzing circulation diagrams. The book asks learners to investigate, document and explain, rather than supplying answers to every activity.
