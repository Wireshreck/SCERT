# Chapter 4 Activities and Assessment

Movement prompts ask learners to identify purposes and mechanisms of movement, label the human skeleton, compare structural frameworks, analyze the synovial-joint diagram and relate exercise to muscle health. The assessment asks about muscle types, joint types, disorders and joint structures. Extended activities include documenting locomotion diversity and preparing an exercise poster.
