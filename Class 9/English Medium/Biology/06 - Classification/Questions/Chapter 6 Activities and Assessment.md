# Chapter 6 Activities and Assessment

Learners complete classification diagrams and tables, compare organism traits, interpret the virus figure, build an evolutionary tree from shared characteristics and investigate DNA barcoding. The work emphasizes evidence-based grouping and the provisional nature of classification as discoveries accumulate.
