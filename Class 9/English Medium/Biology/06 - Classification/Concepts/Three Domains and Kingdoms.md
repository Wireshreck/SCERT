# Three Domains and Kingdoms

Carl Woese’s classification work led to three domains: Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya. The six kingdoms are grouped within those domains. The text distinguishes Bacteria and Archaea using cell-wall composition and other characteristics and introduces animal and plant classification using observable and structural traits.

## Textbook figures and activities

The three-domain diagram groups six kingdoms. A kingdom comparison chart gives characteristics and examples for Bacteria and Archaea; a phyla table begins the classification of animals.
