# Plant Vascular Transport

Xylem conducts water and dissolved mineral materials; phloem conducts food. Water enters root cells by osmosis. Root pressure contributes to upward movement, while transpiration and cohesion/adhesion help draw water through xylem. Photosynthetic glucose is transferred into sieve tubes as sucrose using energy; water entry creates turgor pressure that supports phloem transport.

## Textbook figures and activities

The chapter compares xylem vessels/tracheids with phloem sieve tubes/companion cells and diagrams water ascent and food conduction. A prompt asks about droplets at leaf margins for further investigation.
