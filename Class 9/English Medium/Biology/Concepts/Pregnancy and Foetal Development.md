# Pregnancy and Foetal Development

The chapter presents fertilisation, implantation and foetal development as stages of human reproduction. It discusses pregnancy care and growth across trimesters, with the uterus, placenta and amniotic sac represented in diagrams. Antenatal care, nutrition and health support are presented as important for the well-being of the pregnant person and developing foetus. The chapter also discusses motherhood, breast milk and colostrum.

## Textbook figures and activities

Assessment asks learners to identify full-term pregnancy duration, ultrasound as a way to observe foetal growth, the amniotic sac, implantation and trimester changes. Illustrations show reproductive structures and development.
