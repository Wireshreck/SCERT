# Viruses and Classification

Viruses consist of genetic material (DNA or RNA) inside a protein capsid and lack cytoplasm, organelles and a nucleus. The textbook explains that viruses cannot multiply without a host cell and are inactive outside living cells, so they are not placed in the six-kingdom classification. Virologists classify them in virus-specific groups to study their nature and evolution.
