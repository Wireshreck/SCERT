# Photosynthesis and Plant Nutrition

Photosynthesis uses light energy captured by chlorophyll to synthesize glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen. Glucose can be stored as starch or converted into materials used to form other nutrients. Plants are autotrophs; other organisms obtain nutrients through feeding relationships.

## Textbook figures and activities

The textbook includes a two-phase photosynthesis table and a reactant/product completion task, food-source photographs, ocean light zones and economic/ecological examples of plant services. It asks learners to investigate light conditions rather than supplying a universal outcome for every proposed test.
