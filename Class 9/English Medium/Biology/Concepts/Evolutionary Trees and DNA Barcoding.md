# Evolutionary Trees and DNA Barcoding

An evolutionary tree represents branching relationships and inferred ancestors, using shared characteristics to organize organisms. The textbook’s example compares skull, forelimbs, hair/fur and lactation across lung fish, wall lizard, dog and human. DNA barcoding classifies or identifies organisms by comparing selected DNA sequences shared through research and laboratories. Both approaches support biodiversity study and conservation.

## Textbook figures and activities

Table 6.3 and its tree diagram model how progressively shared traits shape branches. A barcode illustration links animal silhouettes to a DNA helix and a sequence strip.
