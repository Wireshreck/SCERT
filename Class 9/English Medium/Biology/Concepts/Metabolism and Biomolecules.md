# Metabolism and Biomolecules

Metabolism is the total set of chemical reactions in an organism. Anabolism combines molecules; catabolism breaks them down. The textbook groups biomolecules as carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids. Examples shown include glucose and starch; fats and oils; enzymes and antibodies; DNA and RNA. Enzymes accelerate reactions, while hormones coordinate biological processes.
