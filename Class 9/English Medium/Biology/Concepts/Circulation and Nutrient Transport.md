# Circulation and Nutrient Transport

The muscular heart pumps blood through pulmonary and systemic circuits. The four chambers and valves direct one-way flow. A cardiac cycle consists of atrial systole, ventricular systole and joint diastole; the book gives 0.8 s for one cycle. The SA node in the right atrium produces impulses and is called the pacemaker. Pulse is the felt arterial expansion associated with ventricular contraction. The chapter contrasts systolic and diastolic pressure (illustrated as 120/80 mmHg).

## Textbook figures and activities

Heart diagrams identify chambers, valves and major vessels; the cycle figure shows its three phases. An activity compares pulse and heartbeat before and after exercise.
