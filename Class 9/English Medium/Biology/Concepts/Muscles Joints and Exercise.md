# Muscles Joints and Exercise

Muscles and skeletal structures work together to generate movement. The chapter explains that bones contain mineral salts and collagen; osteoblasts deposit minerals and support growth and repair. A synovial joint includes ligaments connecting bones, cartilage reducing friction, and synovial structures that allow smooth movement. Muscle fatigue is introduced in relation to sustained activity and oxygen availability. The assessment covers muscle types, joint movements, exercise and bone/muscle disorders.

## Textbook figures and activities

Figures show muscle fatigue, bone structure, hydroskeleton/exoskeleton/endoskeleton and a synovial joint labeled with ligament, capsule, cartilage, synovial membrane and synovial fluid.
