# Gender Justice and Reproductive Health

Reproductive health is treated together with dignity, equal rights, adolescent education and access to care. Biological sex is described through chromosomes, hormones and physical characteristics, including intersex variations; gender concerns socially shaped identities, expectations and roles. The text argues for equal education, participation, safety, wages and shared family responsibilities and emphasizes reliable, age-appropriate health education.

## Textbook figures and activities

A discussion prompt uses unequal opportunities as a context for gender justice. Textbook examples connect education and public health services with maternal and child well-being.
