# Kidney Function and Urine Formation

The nephron filters blood and adjusts the filtrate. Ultrafiltration at the glomerulus forms glomerular filtrate under pressure. Reabsorption returns needed components from renal tubules to blood; secretion transfers excess substances from blood into the tubules. The resulting urine travels through collecting ducts to the pelvis, then the ureter, bladder and urethra.

## Textbook figures and activities

The nephron flow diagram distinguishes the three stages. A urine-analysis table and Benedict-reagent activity illustrate how components may be investigated; the textbook frames these as learning activities and possible associations.
