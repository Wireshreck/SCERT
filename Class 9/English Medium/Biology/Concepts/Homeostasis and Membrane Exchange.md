# Homeostasis and Membrane Exchange

Homeostasis is maintaining a stable composition of an organism’s internal environment so metabolism can function. The plasma membrane separates cytoplasm from this environment and is selectively permeable: some molecules cross readily while others use specific channels. The textbook defines osmosis as water movement through a semipermeable membrane from higher water concentration to lower water concentration.

## Textbook figures and activities

The cell-membrane diagram shows a phospholipid bilayer and proteins. The paired fresh-water/salt-solution leaf investigation demonstrates water-related cell changes; the beaker diagram models movement across a semipermeable membrane.
