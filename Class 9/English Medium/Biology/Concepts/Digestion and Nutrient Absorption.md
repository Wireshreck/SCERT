# Digestion and Nutrient Absorption

Digestion breaks complex food into simpler absorbable molecules. In the chapter assessment, lipase is associated with fat digestion. The small intestine’s villi provide an absorptive surface with blood capillaries and a lacteal. Glucose and amino acids enter blood capillaries; fatty-acid and glycerol products enter lacteals and lymph vessels. The routes therefore differ before joining systemic circulation.

## Textbook figures and activities

Illustrations of villi and nutrient flow support a labelled route exercise from intestine to liver and heart; assessment questions ask learners to explain why nutrient pathways differ.
