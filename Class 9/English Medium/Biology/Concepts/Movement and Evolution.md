# Movement and Evolution

The chapter connects movement and body structures with adaptation and evolutionary change. Organisms use different frameworks and movement strategies according to their body plans and environments. Human skeletal features are considered alongside other organisms; exercise and observation tasks encourage learners to relate form, function and movement diversity.

## Textbook figures and activities

The chapter ends with assessment questions about skeletal parts, joints and disorders, followed by investigations of locomotion diversity in organisms.
