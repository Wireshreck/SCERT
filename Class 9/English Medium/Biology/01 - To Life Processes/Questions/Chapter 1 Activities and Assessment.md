# Chapter 1 Activities and Assessment

The chapter uses a leaf-cell comparison in fresh water and concentrated salt solution, a semipermeable-membrane water-flow model, and a photosynthesis reactant/product completion diagram. Later tasks ask learners to identify nutrients, investigate photosynthesis under different light sources, explain ocean light zones, and prepare a seminar on the ecological and economic importance of plants. End prompts emphasize plant protection and local biodiversity. Record observations from investigations before drawing inferences; several prompts are explicitly open investigations.
