# Biology Part 2 — Subject Overview

**Standard IX · English Medium · First Edition 2024**

This volume continues Biology Part 1 with movement and body support, reproductive health and human development, and classification as a way to describe biodiversity and evolutionary relationships.

The complete 80-page PDF has been visually reviewed, including chapter pages, diagrams, tables, activity and assessment pages, lined note pages, and civic inserts. Chapter notes and exercise notes are reconciled to the printed page boundaries.

## Chapters

4. [[Chapters/04 - Behind Movements]] — movement in organisms, muscles, skeletons, joints, exercise, disorders and plant movements (printed pp. 87–112).
5. [[Chapters/05 - Reproductive Health]] — gender justice, reproductive health, pregnancy, foetal growth, antenatal care, breastfeeding, vaccination and sexually transmitted infections (pp. 113–138).
6. [[Chapters/06 - Classification]] — domains, kingdoms, animal and plant groups, viruses, evolutionary trees and DNA barcoding (pp. 139–156).
