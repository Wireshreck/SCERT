# Empathy, Communication and Listening

Examines relationships, empathy, communication, listening and cooperation. Activities practise constructive responses, clear expression and attentive listening in peer and community situations. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 39–50.
