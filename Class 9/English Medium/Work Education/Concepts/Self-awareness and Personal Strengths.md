# Self-awareness and Personal Strengths

Activities build self-awareness by identifying abilities, interests, values and areas to improve. Reflection, personal inventories and a Me Chart encourage realistic planning and respect for individual strengths. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 7–15.
