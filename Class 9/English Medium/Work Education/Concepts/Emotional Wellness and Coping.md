# Emotional Wellness and Coping

Explores emotions, stress and emotional well-being through stories, emotion charts, reflection and support-seeking. Students consider healthy responses, coping habits and trusted people who can help. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 30–38.
