# Self-care, Etiquette and Social Conduct

Covers self-care, grooming, etiquette and appropriate conduct in social settings. Scenarios, role-play and reflection connect personal presentation with confidence, respectful interaction and responsibility. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 23–29.
