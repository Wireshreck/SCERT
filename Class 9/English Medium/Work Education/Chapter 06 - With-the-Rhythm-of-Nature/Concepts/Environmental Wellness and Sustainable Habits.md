# Environmental Wellness and Sustainable Habits

Connects well-being with the natural environment and sustainable everyday habits. Learners observe their surroundings, consider environmental impact and make a personal action plan. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 51–60.
