# Resilience and Goal Setting

Uses stories of people who have faced barriers to introduce persistence, resilience and goal-directed effort. Learners identify challenges, possible supports and steps toward progress. The activity book uses observation and guided practice to help learners connect the idea to their own community and creative or personal choices.

**Textbook reference:** Work Integrated Education Standard IX Activity Book Part I — Self Grooming, printed pp. 16–22.
