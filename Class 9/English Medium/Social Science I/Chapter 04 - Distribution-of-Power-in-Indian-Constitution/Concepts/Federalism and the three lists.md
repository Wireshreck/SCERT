# Federalism and the three lists

Union, State and Concurrent lists divide law-making subjects between levels of government. The arrangement is designed to combine national coordination with constitutionally assigned state responsibilities.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part 1, printed pp. 68–75.
