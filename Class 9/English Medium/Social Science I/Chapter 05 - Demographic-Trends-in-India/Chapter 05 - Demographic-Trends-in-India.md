# Demographic Trends in India

Uses population data and census concepts to study change. Indicators include birth and death rates, migration, population density, sex and child sex ratios, life expectancy and age structure. The chapter connects demographic dividend and population policy with planning, while tables and diagrams support comparison and interpretation.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part I, printed pp. 83–100.
