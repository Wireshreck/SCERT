# Sex ratio, life expectancy and demographic dividend

Sex ratio and child sex ratio reveal gendered population patterns; life expectancy reflects mortality conditions. A large working-age population can support a demographic dividend when education, health and jobs enable participation.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part 1, printed pp. 92–100.
