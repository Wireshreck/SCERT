# Mahajanapadas and the Mauryan state

The chapter traces the rise of powerful states, especially Magadha, and Mauryan rule. Chandragupta and Ashoka exemplify political consolidation and administration; the comparison with Greek states raises questions about forms of political participation.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part 1, printed pp. 35–44.
