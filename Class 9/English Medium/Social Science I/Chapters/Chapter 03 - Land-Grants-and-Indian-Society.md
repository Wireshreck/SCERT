# Land Grants and Indian Society

Examines land grants and agrarian expansion and their effects on social hierarchy, crafts and trade. The Gupta period is considered through social life, regional change, temple building, art and scientific achievements. Read the chapter’s illustrations and evidence as examples of how political economy and culture changed together.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part I, printed pp. 45–62.
