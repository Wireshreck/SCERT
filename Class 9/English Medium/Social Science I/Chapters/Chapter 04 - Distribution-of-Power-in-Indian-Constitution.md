# Distribution of Power in Indian Constitution

Introduces the Constitution and its making, including the Objective Resolution. It explains Indian federalism, the Union, State and Concurrent division of responsibilities, and separation of powers among legislature, executive and judiciary. Parliament, checks and balances and constitutional amendment show how authority is distributed and constrained.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part I, printed pp. 63–82.
