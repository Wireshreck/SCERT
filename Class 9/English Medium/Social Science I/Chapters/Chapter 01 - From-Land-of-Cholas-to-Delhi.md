# From Land of Cholas to Delhi

Surveys political and cultural developments from the Cholas and Chalukyas through regional powers such as the Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas, the Arab entry into Sindh, and the Delhi Sultanate. The material relates governance and political change to social, economic and cultural life.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part II, printed pp. 111–128.
