# Moving Forward from the Stone Age

Traces human development through the Palaeolithic, Mesolithic and Neolithic periods and the Metal Ages. Stone tools, cave art, control of fire, settlement, farming and animal domestication mark changing ways of life. The chapter relates archaeological evidence to early civilizations, including Harappan urban life and the Vedic period.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part I, printed pp. 7–24.
