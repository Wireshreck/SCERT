# Towards a Gender-Neutral Society

Distinguishes sex from socially formed gender roles and examines socialisation, stereotypes and inequality. It considers gender-sensitive language, constitutional rights and legal protections, and asks learners to identify practices that support equality in everyday life.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part II, printed pp. 151–166.
