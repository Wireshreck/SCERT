# Extension of Democracy through Institutions

Explains institutions that protect and extend democratic participation: election administration, adult franchise, representation and commissions addressing human, women’s, minority, Scheduled Caste and Tribe, backward-class and child rights. Activities invite learners to consider how independent bodies make rights actionable.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part II, printed pp. 129–150.
