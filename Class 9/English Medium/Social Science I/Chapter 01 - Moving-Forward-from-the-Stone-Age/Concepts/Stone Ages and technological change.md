# Stone Ages and technological change

Palaeolithic, Mesolithic and Neolithic labels describe broad archaeological periods distinguished partly by tool traditions and subsistence patterns. The chapter links changing tools with fire, art, settlement, domestication and farming.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part 1, printed pp. 7–24.
