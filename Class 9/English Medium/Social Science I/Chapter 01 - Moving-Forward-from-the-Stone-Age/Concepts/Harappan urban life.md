# Harappan urban life

The Harappan civilisation is introduced through evidence of planned settlements and material culture. Treat archaeological objects and settlement patterns as evidence from which historians reconstruct social and economic life.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part 1, printed pp. 17–24.
