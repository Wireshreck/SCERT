# Ideas and Early States

Explores social and economic change alongside new religious ideas, including Buddhism and Jainism, and the teachings of Gautama Buddha and Vardhamana Mahavira. It traces the rise of Magadha and the Mahajanapadas, Mauryan rule under Chandragupta and Ashoka, administration and land grants, and compares political institutions such as Greek democracy.

**Textbook reference:** Social Science I — Part I, printed pp. 25–44.
